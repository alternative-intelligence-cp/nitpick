# 0.8.1 — `src/` clean under the real checker, and the fixpoint that closed early

**Cycle 0.8.** The subcycle that was planned as a cleanup and ended as a
milestone: making the compiler clean under its own frontend took it all the way
through its own emitter, and **the stage-1/stage-2 fixpoint closed — byte-identical
— four cycles ahead of the 1.2 line item.**

## The waves, in order

The self-check surfaced debt in strict stage order, each wave masked by the one
before it:

1. **Resolution (78→0).** Cross-module privates (`te_error` ×~30 — `pub` was
   never enforced by the seed's single namespace), missing imports (the 0.7.3
   qualifier move left `QUAL_*` importers pointing at the old module), two
   genuine same-scope duplicates in the pipeline, and one collision the language
   itself adjudicates: a module's own name owns its scope entry, so
   `emit_program.npk` cannot also export `emit_program` — renamed `emit_all`.
2. **Types (256→0).** The `=>` reinterpret-cast debt (148 sites → `=>!`, swept
   to fixpoint as later lines unmasked), `fail Ni32` (~85 sites → `Ntbb32`),
   the `uint8` byte seams, and two real decisions: **D-139** (`[]` is the zero
   array — there was no spelling for a zeroed `T[64]` field) and **D-140**
   (`intN =>! enum` opens: the compiler's own tag round-trips overruled the
   0.8.0 draft within one subcycle).
3. **The architecture bug (D-137).** Fifteen unknown-type findings whose real
   mechanism was that **a declaration's annotations resolved in the asker's
   scope** — `tyres_init(Ast->:…)` typed or failed depending on who called it,
   and under a name collision would have silently bound the caller's type.
   Declarations now record their home at collection; signatures, fields and
   payloads resolve there.
4. **The analyses (32→0, D-138).** One sound narrowing (a type that cannot hold
   an address cannot carry a borrow — returned `int32` fields were flagging) and
   the relaxations D-004's text pre-authorised "with evidence": param-rooted
   borrows travel up one frame compositionally, don't taint bindings, and
   self-wiring (`f.g = graph_init($$m f.it, …)`) is legal because the lifetimes
   are identical by construction. The out-parameter got its spelling for free:
   **`$$m` already credits assignment** in the bindings analysis, so
   `front_run($$m f, root)` says in source what `@f` could not.

## The seed divergence, met in the wild

The first `llc` over the self-emitted compiler refused one string constant:
`esc_byte`, seed-compiled, sign-extended byte 226 — the lead of every em-dash —
because **the seed extends by the target's signedness**, the exact divergence
0.7.5 documented when choosing source-signedness for the real emitter. The fix
masks after the widening, making the function correct under *either* rule — which
is what "correct" has to mean for code that runs under two compilers until the
fixpoint closes. The seed also learned to lower `$$i`/`$$m` (as `@`; mut-ness is
checker-side).

## What closed

```
npkc  src/main.npk            → stage1.ll   (5.5 MB, the whole compiler)
llc + ld.lld                  → npkc₁       (2.0 MB static binary, no seed in it)
npkc₁ tests/…/structs_places  → runs, exit 0
npkc₁ src/main.npk            → stage2.ll
stage1.ll == stage2.ll        → BYTE-IDENTICAL
```

**The harness now runs this on every full invocation** — emit, assemble, link,
re-emit, compare. A drift is either nondeterminism or a semantic divergence
between what the seed built and what the compiler builds; both are stop-the-line.
This does not retire the seed (that is still 1.2's formal work: pinned stages, a
committed `stage1.ll`, the bootstrap-from-nothing story) — it makes the fixpoint
a standing instrument from the day it first closed.

## Done when

- The fixpoint stage is green in the full harness beside every existing suite —
  the analyses' rejection tests still hold their lines after D-138.
