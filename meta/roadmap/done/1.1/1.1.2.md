# 1.1.2 — The `tests/` sweep, then `raw` and `drop` refuse (D-163 closed)

**Cycle 1.1.** The last third of D-163: the suites are brought under the
licence, the rule becomes a refusal, and the instrument is retired.

## The worklist

`tests/`: ~3,500 `raw` sites and ~280 `drop` sites, ~1,070 of them on may-fail
callees — which collapse to a few dozen shared helpers (`diaglist_init`,
`lex_all`, `parser_init`, `ast_init`, `exprtypes_set`, `fnem_begin` …); 186
bare statements already keyworded in 1.1.0. The same rewrite rule as 1.1.1,
with one addition: a test that *deliberately* exercises a failure keeps the
error visible (`is_err` + `exit`), never `? NIL`.

## The flip

With every tree's count at zero:

1. `type_unwrap`'s `raw` and `drop` arms resolve the operand's callee
   (`callee_decl`; the trait's declaration for `dyn`; the function type's flag
   for a pointer; the builtin column) and refuse anything unlicensed with
   `TYPE_RAW_UNLICENSED`; `drop` additionally requires the success type `NIL`
   and exempts the spawn form (an un-awaited call to an `async` function —
   which 1.1's coroutine subcycles then wire to the D-062 join).
2. The rejection suite gains `TYPE_RAW_UNLICENSED`'s cases: a may-fail callee
   under `raw`; under `drop`; a binding; a builtin without the column; a `dyn`
   call whose trait does not declare it; a function-pointer call whose type
   does not; `drop` of a never-failing value-returning callee. The acceptance
   suite covers every licensed form — direct, UFCS, trait method on a concrete
   receiver, `dyn` whose trait declares it, function pointer of marked type,
   builtin from the column, generic instantiation, comptime call, `drop` of a
   `NIL` never-fails callee, `drop work()` on an `async` callee (accepted by
   the checker; rung-refused by the backend until the executor lands).
3. `check_raw_licensed` becomes a **failing** check — a planted regression
   fails the harness by file and line — and is then **retired in the same
   commit**: an instrument that duplicates a checker is two representations of
   one fact, the recurring defect. Its job was the measurement.
4. The `selfhost` stage runs `src/` under the refusing checker; the fixpoint is
   re-proven.

## Done when

- `raw` / `drop` on anything unlicensed is refused with its code and span;
  every licensed form is accepted.
- `check_raw_licensed` reported zero everywhere, failed on a planted
  regression, and is gone.
- `SAFETY_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s Escape Hatches list no longer names `raw`; the
  `Result` discipline has no silent sink; `check_codes_tested` sees all six
  codes cased.
- The fixpoint holds; D-163's status line reads **SETTLED**.

## Outcome

**The licence is ON.** An unlicensed `raw` or `drop` is refused with
`NITPICK-TYPE-042` at its span; every licensed form is accepted; the
`selfhost` stage proves `src/` under the refusing checker with the fixpoint
byte-identical; and the `Result` discipline has no silent sink left.

### The tests sweep, then the flip

- The 1.1.1 tooling pointed at `tests/`: proposer + scoped relay→raw sweeps
  (scope-correct after a found hazard: test files reuse helper names, and a
  mark in ONE file must never license a different file's may-fail local — the
  sweeper now licenses only src/lib-global or same-file names). 448 test-local
  helpers marked, all checker-audited; the ~140-site hand-read tail spelled
  `?! 9tbb32` — a test's unexpected failure aborts LOUDLY — with `is_err`
  kept where failure is the thing under test.
- **The flip**: `type_unwrap`'s `raw`/`drop` arms resolve the operand's callee
  the way the instrument did — the declaration (direct/UFCS; the TRAIT's
  declaration for `dyn`, which 1.0.5's `find_method` already recorded), the
  function type's flag (indirect), the builtin column — and refuse
  `TYPE_RAW_UNLICENSED` otherwise. `drop` additionally requires the success
  type `NIL` (the four void builtins by name, since a builtin types as
  unknown), with the spawn form exempt (an un-awaited `async` callee, D-058).
- Rule 4's `discard(raw f());` rewrite: **~350 sites** (the decision's ~90
  estimate, ×4 — the cost was known and taken; `p_expect`'s bool and
  `te_error`'s int32 are most of it).
- The rejection/acceptance pair: `raw_unlicensed.npk` holds all SEVEN
  refusals (may-fail under `raw` and `drop`; a binding; a may-fail builtin;
  a `dyn` whose trait does not declare; an unmarked function type; `drop` of
  a value) and `raw_licensed.npk` every licensed form plus the spawn.

### The instrument, proven and retired

The planted regression was caught — better than designed: the REFUSING
CHECKER rejected the plant (`paths.npk:202:48`, by file and line) before the
reporting instrument could even run its root, which is the retirement argument
made concrete. `check_raw_licensed` and `check --raw-report` are gone in this
same commit; two representations of one fact was the recurring defect.

### Two more compiler bugs found (the flip's audit dividend, #4 and #5)

4. **Extern calls double-wrapped**: `extern_fn_type` stored the WRAPPED
   `Result` as its success slot — the same bug 0.9.6 fixed for ordinary
   functions, missed on the extern path since 0.7 — so every extern call
   typed as `Result<Result<T>>` to its unwrappers. Invisible until the flip's
   `?!` handed back a still-wrapped Result.
5. **The lock-level analysis was blind to `?!`/`?|`**: `init_acquires`
   unwrapped only the one-operand operators, and the ?-family is two-operand
   — an acquisition under them was silently invisible since 0.9.7, exactly
   the "under-approximate SILENTLY" failure D-056 warns of. Found when the
   sweep first spelled a lock constructor `take_1() ?! c` and three LOCK-001s
   vanished.

Also closed: the emitter's `?!`/`?|` over a BUILTIN operand (typed unknown)
now derives the Result layout from the operand's own LLVM type — the last
gap between the ?-family and the floor.

### D-163, end to end

Opened as G-4 — "the last claims the compiler took on faith" — and closed
with the claims checked: `never fails` on ~2,400 functions (src, prelude,
tests), 44 accessors trapping instead of failing-to-be-swallowed, the
statement forms a closed list, the licence refusing, and **five real defects**
found by the sweeps that nothing else had ever reached. The Escape-Hatches
list no longer names `raw`.
