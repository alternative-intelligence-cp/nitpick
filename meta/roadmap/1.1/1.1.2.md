# 1.1.2 — The `tests/` sweep, then `raw` and `drop` refuse (D-163 closed)

**Cycle 1.1.** The last third of D-163: the suites are brought under the
licence, the rule becomes a refusal, and the instrument is retired.

## The worklist

`tests/`: ~3,500 `raw` sites and ~280 `drop` sites, ~1,070 of them on may-fail
callees — which collapse to a few dozen shared helpers (`diaglist_init`,
`lex_all`, `parser_init`, `ast_init`, `exprtypes_set`, `fnem_begin` …); 186
bare statements already keyworded in 1.1.0. The same rewrite rule as 1.1.1,
with one addition: a test that *deliberately* exercises a failure keeps the
error visible (`is_err` + `exit`), never `? NIL`.

## The flip

With every tree's count at zero:

1. `type_unwrap`'s `raw` and `drop` arms resolve the operand's callee
   (`callee_decl`; the trait's declaration for `dyn`; the function type's flag
   for a pointer; the builtin column) and refuse anything unlicensed with
   `TYPE_RAW_UNLICENSED`; `drop` additionally requires the success type `NIL`
   and exempts the spawn form (an un-awaited call to an `async` function —
   which 1.1's coroutine subcycles then wire to the D-062 join).
2. The rejection suite gains `TYPE_RAW_UNLICENSED`'s cases: a may-fail callee
   under `raw`; under `drop`; a binding; a builtin without the column; a `dyn`
   call whose trait does not declare it; a function-pointer call whose type
   does not; `drop` of a never-failing value-returning callee. The acceptance
   suite covers every licensed form — direct, UFCS, trait method on a concrete
   receiver, `dyn` whose trait declares it, function pointer of marked type,
   builtin from the column, generic instantiation, comptime call, `drop` of a
   `NIL` never-fails callee, `drop work()` on an `async` callee (accepted by
   the checker; rung-refused by the backend until the executor lands).
3. `check_raw_licensed` becomes a **failing** check — a planted regression
   fails the harness by file and line — and is then **retired in the same
   commit**: an instrument that duplicates a checker is two representations of
   one fact, the recurring defect. Its job was the measurement.
4. The `selfhost` stage runs `src/` under the refusing checker; the fixpoint is
   re-proven.

## Done when

- `raw` / `drop` on anything unlicensed is refused with its code and span;
  every licensed form is accepted.
- `check_raw_licensed` reported zero everywhere, failed on a planted
  regression, and is gone.
- `SAFETY_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s Escape Hatches list no longer names `raw`; the
  `Result` discipline has no silent sink; `check_codes_tested` sees all six
  codes cased.
- The fixpoint holds; D-163's status line reads **SETTLED**.
