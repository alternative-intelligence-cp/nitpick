## The rule

A binding moved anywhere in a loop body is MOVED-FROM at the body's head
unless it is reassigned on EVERY path that reaches the back edge. The
`modmap_members` shape (one `move(x)` re-executed per iteration = N
moves of one value = N drops of one body) refuses with its own code.
