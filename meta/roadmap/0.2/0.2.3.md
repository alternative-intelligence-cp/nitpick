# 0.2.3 — Expressions

**Cycle 0.2 — AST and parser.**

## Goal

The whole expression grammar, at the precedence of `OP_REFERENCE.md` §0.

## The table has 19 levels, and level 2 is new

`raw`, `drop`, `await` and `relay` occupy **level 2** (D-081) — looser than
postfix so `raw a.eq(b)` takes the whole call, tighter than cast and pipeline so
it is the *value* that gets cast or piped, a `Result` being meaningless to
either. They had no level at all before D-081, and getting this wrong changes
what programs mean rather than failing.

## Work

1. Precedence climbing over the 19 levels.
2. Postfix chains — call, index, field, `?.`, turbofish `::<T>`.
3. **Turbofish is the only expression-position form** for explicit type arguments
   (D-064). Bare `f<T>(x)` is not a generic call.
4. Literals, including templates: `TemplateStart` … `InterpStart` expr
   `InterpEnd` … `TemplateEnd` becomes an interpolation node.
5. `move(place)`, `comptime(expr)` — keyword operators with a parenthesised
   operand (D-065).

## Done when

- Every expression form in `AST_REFERENCE.md` §3 parses at the right precedence.
- Associativity is tested, not assumed.
