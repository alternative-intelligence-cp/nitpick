## What it builds

- **D-165 — module-level bindings.** A constant initialiser lowers to an LLVM
  `constant`/`global` under D-156's symbol; a call in one is refused
  (`TYPE_GLOBAL_NOT_CONSTANT`); `a module-level binding` and `a name that is
  not a local` retire.
- **D-168 — `&{ }` through `ToString`.** The prelude's `impl:…:ToString` for
  every builtin scalar over floor conversions (`npk_int_to_string` and
  siblings, one per family); the template lowers to a `string_concat` chain;
  a type with no impl is refused at the checker (`TYPE_NOT_STRINGABLE`);
  `a template literal` retires.
- **D-166 — `for` over an `Iterator`.** `Self->` admitted as a receiver in a
  trait signature (`check_signature` substitutes through the receiver's
  wrapper; object safety treats it as a receiver); the prelude's `Iterator`;
  the loop lowering (`next(@it)` until `NIL`); the binding's type must equal
  `Item`. `a `for` over this iterable` retires.
- **D-123's two generators**: `#[derive(Hash)]` (FNV-1a over every field, a
  `string` through its bytes) and `#[derive(Ord)]` on an enum (the tag
  through `=>! int32`); `DERIVE_NOT_GENERATED` retires with them.
