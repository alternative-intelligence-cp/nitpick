## 1. Where it starts (measured 2026-09-10 on `12a6a78`; the probes are in the session's scratchpad `p4b/`, to be committed as `tests/verify/` programs as the steps land)

**The shift-amount probe (the skeleton's first item) — a soundness defect.**
`func:sh = int32(int32:x, int32:n) never fails { pass (x << n); }` called
with `n = 40` passes the checker silently (`type_bitwise` unifies the two
operand types and asks nothing of the amount) and the emitter writes a bare
`%t4 = shl i32 %t2, %t3` — LLVM's `shl`/`lshr`/`ashr` by an amount at or past
the width is POISON. The run exited 0 by coincidence (x86 masks the count).
The constant folder (`fold_binary`, `type_resolve.npk`) refuses to fold a
distance `< 0` or `>= 64` — the right instinct, the wrong bound: an `int32`
shifted by 40 folds to `2^40` stored in an `int32` constant. The
specification says what a shift does and nothing about an amount outside
`0..width-1` (OP_REFERENCE §4's two rows; D-210: "shifts and bitwise ops are
BIT operations and are unchanged"). The prototype had the same hole and a
NIKOS warning for it (`nikos_warning_promoter.cpp`: "shift amount out of
range"). No emitted guard, no row, no reach arming. **S-52** settles the
semantics; step 0 lands them.

**Where the shifts are.** Non-literal amounts in the compiler proper: three,
all in `types.npk`'s unit-vector packing (`packed >> ((axis * 8) => int64)`,
`255 << sh`, `by << sh`); in the prelude eleven (the float `ToString`'s
`1u64 << mant_bits`, `bits >> mant_bits`, `(F << ew) << 1u2048`, `1u2048 <<
nw`…, and `tfp_render`'s `1u256 << (fbits => uint256)`, `t >> (fbits =>
uint256)`), of which `tfp_render` is emitted in the compiler's own IR (it is
among the six prelude symbols the compiler's manifest already holds); the
libraries' and tests' shifts are literal-amount almost without exception.

**The obligation walk covers the EMITTED functions only.** The compiler's own
`rows.txt` names 46 symbols, six of them prelude functions (`ByteReader.seek`,
`npk_frac_norm`, `npk_gcd256`, `text_write_str<ByteWriter>`, `tfp_render`,
`uint_to_decimal`) — the ones D-262's trim keeps (107 kept). So the rows a
new theory adds to `nitpick.obligations` come from those functions, not from
the prelude's 348 generated impls: `dim256`'s 725 generated compares produce
no row anywhere until a program references one.

**The manifest's tier column already exists and is a constant.** A row is
`<hash> <kind> <tier> <verdict> <elision> <symbol>` (P-10) and both runners
write `int` for every encoded row and `-` for `unencoded`/`checker`
(`harness.py:3166`, `npkg/verify.npk:248`); `rows.txt` carries no tier. D-218
(5)'s "the manifest recording which tier discharged what" is that column
fed from the encoder (§2 step 3).

**The `unencoded` producers left:** `record_unencoded_division` (a `simd`
division's any-lane guard, one caller) and `record_limit_unencoded` (a
`limit` write point whose subject has no term — a twisted or float subject
today). Steps 2, 3 and 4 remove all three sources.

**z3 4.16.0 under the profile (`smt.random_seed=0 sat.random_seed=0
rlimit=20000000`), the crossing itself measured** — s3's §4b measured pure
QF_BV; these mix `Int` and bit-vectors through `(_ int2bv w)`/`bv2nat`
under `(set-logic ALL)`, the way the encoder will:

Three conclusions the design rests on. (1) The crossing is free at 32 and 64
bits when an operand is a numeral, costs 4% of the budget per row at 128,
18% at 256, and exhausts it at 2048 — s3's "the exact width is affordable"
held for the bit-vector theory alone, not for the crossing into it. (2) The
shapes the tree actually writes at the wide widths — a shift by a literal, a
mask of low bits, a bit test against a literal — are pure Int arithmetic
(`*`, `div`, `mod` by a power of two), trivial at any width, and need no
crossing at all. (3) A general bitwise operation with two unknown operands
can exhaust the budget even at 32 bits when an Int inequality reads the
result — a `budget` verdict, safe and unproven; the design must not let
such a term into the cone of a row that is discharged today.

**The prelude's twisted-kind operator sites** (blocks whose signature names
the family; a regex ceiling, comments and strings stripped): `tbb` — NO
arithmetic, 6 compares, 6 `=>!`, 4 `=>`, 10 `is_err` (the `Hash`, `ToString`,
`Eq`/`Ord` impls); `tfp` — 12 `+`, 24 `−`, 34 `*`, 21 `/`, 70 compares, 12
`=>!`, 12 `is_err`, 10 `ERR` (the render, the complex cores); `dim256` — 725
compares in 100 generated blocks and nothing else; the ternary family — 20
compares, 4 `=>!`, 4 `=>`; `frac` and `complex` — compares only (their
arithmetic is the prelude's `int256`/per-element cores, plain integers the
manifest already covers); floats — 16 `+`, 20 `−`, 24 `*`, 12 `/`, 60
compares, 4 `=>!` (Dragon4 and the complex float bodies). The compiler
proper has no twisted, float, `simd`, `frac` or `complex` site (§4b).

**The emitter's twisted arithmetic, which the encoding mirrors exactly
(1.3.2's record, `ir_expr.npk`):** the carrier is the native `iN`, ERR is
the carrier's `INT_MIN` (`tbb_min_decimal`); add/sub/negation/`%` through
the tbb machinery (any ERR operand or an out-of-range result is ERR, the
taint sticky and branch-free); `tfp` multiply widens to `2N`, multiplies,
`ashr F` (floor), narrows with a round-trip check (`tfp_narrow`: a result
whose sign-extension differs, or that equals the sentinel, is ERR); `tfp`
divide: a zero divisor joins the taint (no trap — "twisted division arms no
DivByZero"), the dividend widened and shifted up by `F`, `sdiv`
(truncation), the same narrow; `tbb_divlike` the same without the scale;
every comparison on a twisted operand TRAPS on ERR (`-4100`, `TbbErr`,
before the compare); every cast OUT of the family traps on ERR under both
spellings (`tbb_trap_if`, D-144 as amended); `dim256` is `tfp256` by
identity; the ternary kinds store the VALUE with balanced bounds and the
same sentinel. The encoder has no arm for any of it: a bitwise, twisted,
float or `simd` operand makes the term opaque (`term_opaque`), so a row
over one is `open` or `unencoded`.

## 2. What lands (six steps; each under D-228; the manifest re-recorded in every commit that moves it, D-040, with the delta stated in the record)

### Step 0 — S-52: a shift amount is checked (`ShiftRange`, `shift-range`)

- **The rule (S-52 as recommended).** `x << n` and `x >> n` are defined for
  `0 ≤ n < width(x)` and for no other amount. An amount the compiler KNOWS —
  a literal, or an expression the folder folds — outside that range is
  `NITPICK-TYPE-070` at the shift (TYPE_SHIFT_RANGE; both spellings, the
  compound `<<=`/`>>=` through `type_stmt`'s mapping to the plain operator);
  an amount it does not know is checked at run time: `icmp ult n, W` on the
  amount's carrier — ONE unsigned compare covers a negative amount too,
  since a negative reads as `≥ 2^(W−1) ≥ W` — and the false branch traps
  `ShiftRange` (`-4115`; the prelude's `pub error:ShiftRange = 4115i32;`,
  regenerated into `prelude_source.npk` by `gen_tables.py`). The value of an
  in-range shift is unchanged (D-210: a bit operation, no overflow trap). The
  folder's bound becomes the TYPE's width in both directions (it was `64`
  for every width); the compile-time evaluator's shift arm — the second
  evaluator DEF-29 teaches to keep in step; the executor finds it by its
  counted-loop arm, since no `eval_`-shaped name matched — applies the same
  rule and refuses (an evaluation error, never a value) outside the range.
- **The row.** `OK_SHIFT_RANGE = 19` (`OK_COUNT` 19), name `shift-range`,
  `ok_has_guard` true; the catalogue table's row in VERIFICATION_REFERENCE
  ("a shift's computed amount is inside `0..width-1` (S-52): the compare
  before the shift, `ShiftRange`; a known amount is the checker's (TYPE-070)
  and has no row" — `check_obligation_kinds_agree` diffs the two lists);
  `Z3_KINDS` and `TRAP_OF_KIND` (`-4115`) in harness.py, `ASSUME_KINDS`
  gains it (the elision is one `llvm.assume(icmp ult n, W)`), npkg's
  `trap_of_kind`/`kind_of_trap` and its codes list. The encoder records it
  at every shift whose amount is not a numeral term: `record(e,
  OK_SHIFT_RANGE, (and (>= n 0) (< n W)), n.syms, SITE_EXPR, site, at, true)`
  — a `record_shift` beside `record_division` — and pushes the same
  conjunction as a hypothesis after the site (the guard's fact, as
  `div-zero`'s `b ≠ 0` is). The shift's own TERM stays opaque in this step
  (step 1 encodes it). The emitter (`emit_arith_value`, both spellings since
  1.3.3) emits the compare-and-trap before `shl`/`lshr`/`ashr` when the
  amount is not a literal, and the `assume` when the row is discharged
  (`llctx_elided`, as the division guard). The reach analysis sets
  `saw_shift` at such a shift and `reach_settle` arms `ShiftRange`.
- **Tests.** `tests/types/rejection/shift_literal.npk`: TYPE-070 ×4 (`<<`
  by the width, `>>` past it, a negative literal, `<<=`); `tests/backend/
  programs/shift_range.npk` (computed in-range amounts through both
  operators and both signednesses, exit through the values; `failsafe` names
  `ShiftRange`); `shift_trap.npk` (a computed out-of-range amount, and a
  negative one, each trapping to `failsafe`'s `(ShiftRange)` arm — two
  programs or one with `argv` choosing); `tests/verify/shift_range.npk` (an
  unconstrained amount: `shift-range open 1`; a `limit<r_lt32>` amount:
  `shift-range discharged 1`; a `requires`-bounded one discharged; the
  `failsafe-post` rows named); the fold: `tests/accept/folding.npk` gains a
  folded in-range shift, `shift_literal.npk` a folded out-of-range one
  (`fixed int32:S = 40i32; … 1i32 << S` — TYPE-070 through the folder).
  The compiler's own manifest gains the rows of `types.npk`'s three sites
  and `tfp_render`'s two — re-recorded in this commit with each verdict
  stated (they will be `open`: the amounts are parameters nothing bounds).

### Step 1 — S-54, S-55: bitwise operations encoded — Int forms first, the crossing bounded

- **Int forms (no crossing, any width), when the shape allows:** `~x` →
  `(- (- x) 1)` for a signed type, `(- (2^W − 1) x)` unsigned; `x << k`
  with `k` a numeral → `(mod (* x 2^k) 2^W)` for unsigned, and for signed
  `s = (mod (* x 2^k) 2^W)`, `(ite (>= s 2^(W−1)) (- s 2^W) s)` (the wrap
  the machine's `shl` performs, measured 1,287 rlimit at 64 bits); `x >> k`
  numeral → `(div x 2^k)` (SMT-LIB `div` by a positive numeral is floor
  division, which is `ashr` on a signed value and `lshr` on an unsigned
  one); `x & m` with `m` a numeral `2^j − 1` → `(mod x 2^j)` (two's-complement
  low bits are the mathematical residue); `x & m` with `m` a numeral `2^j`
  → `(* (mod (div x 2^j) 2) 2^j)`; `x & ~(2^j − 1)` (a numeral clearing the
  low bits) → `(- x (mod x 2^j))`. These cover the tree's wide-width sites
  (Dragon4, `tfp_render`, the FNV bodies' literal shifts) at 504–644 rlimit
  regardless of width.
- **The crossing, for every other shape at `type_int_bits ≤ BV_CROSS_MAX_BITS
  = 64`:** `(bv2nat (bvop ((_ int2bv W) a) ((_ int2bv W) b)))` for `& | ^`
  and for `<<`/`>>` by a non-numeral amount (`bvshl`/`bvlshr`/`bvashr`, the
  amount widened to `W` bits — meaningful only under step 0's hypothesis
  `0 ≤ n < W`, which the row guarantees), re-signed for a signed result:
  `(ite (>= r 2^(W−1)) (- r 2^W) r)`. `int2bv` of a negative Int is its
  two's-complement pattern (the SMT-LIB definition is `x mod 2^W`), so the
  crossing is exact for signed operands. Above 64 bits a general operation
  stays OPAQUE (`term_opaque`, as today) with the reason a comment beside
  the constant: the 128-bit crossing costs 884K rlimit per row, 256 costs
  3.6M, 2048 the budget. The constant is one place, and moving it is a
  measurement, not a design change. The flag families (`oflags`, `prot`,
  `mflags`, `fmode`, D-230): `|` and `&` cross at their carrier width (32),
  `~` the Int form; a member constant is a numeral (the prelude declares
  them), so a flag test is the numeral shape.
- **The logic line:** `(set-logic ALL)` when any bit-vector or floating-point
  term appears in the function's file; the existing four-way choice
  (`QF_LIA`/`QF_NIA`/`QF_UF…`) otherwise, so a function no theory touches
  emits the byte-identical file it emits today — the canonical text of a row
  is its cone, which the logic line is not part of, so no untouched hash
  moves.
- **The gate:** every row of `nitpick.obligations` that is `discharged` on
  `12a6a78` is `discharged` after this step (a term newly in a cone can only
  ADD facts; a `budget` where a `discharged` was would be a regression the
  record must explain or the step must revert). The record states the rows
  whose verdicts moved `open → discharged` (a masked divisor, a bit test in
  a division's cone) and the compiler's walk time before and after.
- **Tests (`tests/verify/`):** `divz_masked.npk` (the skeleton's: `n / ((k &
  6i32) | 2i32)` — `div-zero discharged` through the crossing at 32 bits),
  `divz_shift_lit.npk` (`n / (1i32 << 3i32)` and `n / (m >> 2i32 | 1i32)`
  through the Int forms), `divz_wide_mask.npk` (a `uint2048` low-bits mask
  divisor — the Int form at the wide width, discharged), `bit_test.npk` (a
  flag word tested by a numeral bit in a division's path condition),
  `bv_budget.npk` (two unknowns under `&` read by an inequality: the row
  reads `div-zero budget 1` — the limit documented as an expectation),
  `shift_range.npk` grows a `div-zero discharged` over a shifted divisor
  under a `limit`ed amount.

### Step 2 — S-53: the twisted kinds as scaled Int with ERR a value; `err-exit` rows live

- **The value model.** A `tbb`/`tfp`/`dim256`/`trit`/`tryte`/`nit`/`nyte`
  expression is an Int term in the carrier's range with `ERR = MIN(width)`
  as a distinguished value (`smt_int_min`): `ERR` → `MIN`; `is_err(x)` →
  `(= x MIN)`; a literal → the carrier value the emitter folds (a `tfp`
  literal's Q-scaled integer from `tfp_literal_bits`, exact); the range
  axiom of every twisted symbol is `MIN ≤ x ≤ MAX` (ERR included — it IS a
  value, D-008). The operations, each an `ite` in the emitter's exact
  shape: `a + b` → `(ite (or (= a MIN) (= b MIN) (< (+ a b) (+ MIN 1)) (> (+
  a b) MAX)) MIN (+ a b))`, `−`, unary `−` and `%` alike (D-008's
  saturate-to-ERR, the taint sticky); `tbb`'s `*` the same over `(* a b)`
  (`nonlinear` set); `tfp` `*` → `p = (div (* a b) 2^F)` — SMT `div` by the
  positive numeral is floor, which is `ashr F` — then the narrow `(ite (or
  taint (< p (+ MIN 1)) (> p MAX)) MIN p)`; `tfp` `/` → `(ite (or taint (= b
  0)) MIN narrow(npk_sdiv (* a 2^F) b))` (truncation, the define-fun the
  encoder already emits); `tbb` `/` and `%` → the same without the scale;
  `dim256` is `tfp256` (one kind of term; the unit is the type's); the
  ternary kinds: `MIN`/`MAX` are the balanced bounds `∓(3^k−1)/2` around
  the carrier's sentinel (the same `tbb_min_decimal` family), the digit
  operations `&`/`|` on `trit`/`nit` are `min`/`max` as `ite`, and
  `.trit(i)`/`.nit(i)` stay opaque. A twisted division records NO row (it
  never traps: a zero divisor is ERR).
- **The rows: `err-exit` (kind 14, in the catalogue since 1.5.0 "from
  1.5.8" — from this step), one per `-4100` guard, three shapes:** a
  comparison `a < b` (etc.) on twisted operands → the row `(and (not (= a
  MIN)) (not (= b MIN)))`, the compare's term `(< a b)` after it and the
  row's fact a hypothesis; a cast OUT of the family (`=>` and `=>!` alike,
  D-144 as amended) → `(not (= a MIN))`, the value's term the operand's
  Int when the target is an integer (a `cast-range` row over the crossing's
  width is 1.5.8's), opaque when it is a float; the `?!`-shaped exits the
  emitter guards through `tbb_trap_if` in the cast matrix are those two.
  `TRAP_OF_KIND` gains `err-exit: -4100` in both runners; `ASSUME_KINDS`
  gains it (the elision: `llvm.assume` of the not-ERR conjunction at the
  site). The reach analysis already arms `TbbErr` for any twisted
  expression; unchanged.
- **`limit` over a twisted subject** encodes now (`record_limit_unencoded`'s
  twisted case ends): the rule's `$` is the Int term, and `is_err($)` in a
  rule is `(= $ MIN)`.
- **Tests:** `twisted_cmp.npk` (a compare after `if (!is_err(a) && !is_err(b))`:
  `err-exit discharged 1`; an unguarded compare: `err-exit open 1`),
  `twisted_cast.npk` (`=>` and `=>!` out of `tbb32`, guarded and not),
  `tfp_mul_range.npk` (a `tfp64` product under a `limit` on both operands
  whose range stays in bounds: a later `=>!` discharged), `tfp_div_err.npk`
  (a zero divisor is ERR — no row, the ERR reaching a compare's row),
  `dim256_cmp.npk` (the unit type compared: the same row), `tern_cmp.npk`
  (a `tryte` compare), `limit_tbb.npk` (a `limit<r_pos> tbb32` write:
  `limit discharged`, where today it is `unencoded`). The compiler's own
  manifest gains the `err-exit` rows of `tfp_render` (its compares and
  `=>!`s) — counted and re-recorded in this commit.

### Step 3 — S-56: floats — tier 1 in QF_FP, the tier column fed by the encoder, tier 2 as the Real-interval abstraction

- **Sorts.** `flt32` → `(_ FloatingPoint 8 24)`, `flt64` → `(_ FloatingPoint
  11 53)`; the `Term` gains a sort tag (`SORT_INT`, `SORT_BOOL`, `SORT_FP32`,
  `SORT_FP64`, `SORT_REAL`; `is_bool` reads it) so a float term is never
  handed to an Int operator; a symbol's declaration and its range axiom
  follow the sort (a float symbol has no range axiom).
- **Tier 1 terms.** A literal → its exact IEEE pattern from the emitter's
  own folded bits, `(fp #b<sign> #b<exp> #b<mant>)` — never a decimal the
  solver rounds; `+ − * /` → `fp.add/sub/mul/div RNE`; unary `−` → `fp.neg`;
  `#sqrt` → `fp.sqrt RNE`; `<`/`<=`/`>`/`>=` → `fp.lt/leq/gt/geq`; `==` →
  `fp.eq`, `!=` → `(not (fp.eq …))` — the executor reads the emitter's
  `fcmp` predicates (`oeq`/`une`/`olt`…) and encodes the SAME NaN
  behaviour, per operator, as a table in the record; `%` (`frem`, a
  truncated fmod, NOT SMT-LIB's IEEE `fp.rem`) stays opaque; `int => flt` →
  `((_ to_fp eb sb) RNE (to_real x))`; a cast OUT of a float (`=>!` to an
  integer, a `flt64 =>! flt32`) stays opaque until 1.5.8's `cast-range`
  rows. **From step 2:** the `err-exit` row at a checked crossing from a
  float INTO a twisted family (`flt64 => tbb32`, `=> tryte`) is recorded
  `unencoded` by `record_unencoded_err_exit`'s float case; this step gives
  it its goal over the float term — the emitter's strict ordered bounds,
  `(and (fp.gt f LO) (fp.lt f HI))`, false for NaN as the `fcmp` is — and
  the value entering the family `((_ fp.to_sbv W) RTZ f)` read as an Int, so
  the row moves `unencoded` → `open`/`discharged` and the tier reads `fp`.
  No new row kind: floats never trap (D-007); what tier 1 buys is
  that a `limit` over a float subject, a contract clause, an `invariant`
  and a `prove` mentioning floats are encoded rows (today `unencoded` or
  `open` over an opaque term).
- **The tier column.** `rows.txt` gains a `tier` field (the executor extends
  the ten-field line and both runners' parsers): `int` for a cone with
  Int/Bool sorts only, `bv` when a bit-vector crossing is in the cone, `fp`
  when a float sort is, `-` for `unencoded`/`checker`; both runners write
  the field to the manifest's column in place of the constant. A tier-2
  discharge is written `real` by the RUNNER (below). The compiler's own
  rows all read `int` before and after (no float in `src/`), so the
  manifest does not move for the column alone.
- **Tier 2, the Real-interval abstraction (D-218 (5) verbatim: "Real-interval
  abstraction for heavy non-linear … undischarged = retained runtime
  guard").** For every row whose cone holds a float sort the encoder ALSO
  writes a second query, `NNNN.t2.smt2` (QF_NRA, one `(check-sat)` per such
  row, the same ordinals), in which every float term is a Real: each float
  SYMBOL `x` becomes a Real `x_r`; each operation's result is a fresh Real
  `r` with the axiom `|r − v| ≤ ε·|v| + η` (`abs` as `ite`; `v` the exact
  Real of the operation, `ε = 2^-53`/`2^-24`, `η = 2^-1074`/`2^-149` —
  the standard model of a correctly rounded operation, subnormals covered
  by `η`; `fp.sqrt` the same over `√v`, spelled `r² ∈ [v(1−ε)², v(1+ε)²]`,
  `r ≥ 0`); a comparison is the Real comparison. THREE CONDITIONS make it
  sound, and the encoder writes a tier-2 query only when all three hold,
  else no `.t2` row: (i) every float symbol in the cone is bounded below AND
  above by hypotheses that are float comparisons against numerals (a
  `limit` rule, a `requires` clause, a path condition) — such a hypothesis
  is false for NaN and, with both bounds, for ±∞, so the abstraction's
  "inputs are finite reals" premise is the hypotheses' own; (ii) every
  intermediate's magnitude is proven within the normal range: `|v| ≤
  MAX_NORMAL` for each operation is CONJOINED TO THE GOAL, so an `unsat`

## 3. Questions for the user (each with its recommendation; the S-numbers are minted in OPEN_DECISIONS §2e)

- **S-52 — what a shift by an amount outside `0..width−1` means.** Today:
  poison reaches LLVM (the probe). Options: TRAP (`ShiftRange`, a known
  amount refused at compile time, a computed one guarded and a `shift-range`
  row — the language's posture for division, overflow, bounds and the
  counted loop's step), MASK (`n mod width`, x86's habit — silently a
  different shift than the author wrote), SATURATE (`0`, or `−1` for a
  signed right shift — mathematically the limit of the shift, silently a
  different shift than the author wrote). **Recommendation: TRAP**, with the
  folder's bound the type's width and every evaluator agreeing.
- **S-53 — the ERR-sentinel rows.** A new kind per twisted operation, or the
  sentinel as a value feeding the EXISTING guards' rows? Counted: the only
  twisted-kind TRAP is `-4100` (`TbbErr`), at a comparison and at a cast
  out of the family; a twisted division never traps. **Recommendation: no
  new kind** — `err-exit` (kind 14, in the catalogue since 1.5.0) is the
  row at every `-4100` guard, ERR is a value the terms carry, and D-218.7's
  "every carried guard appears" holds with one kind per trap identity,
  which keeps both runners' trap table a function.
- **S-54 — which bitwise operations cross.** All five and `~`, plus the flag
  families' `|`/`&`/`~` — with the Int forms for the numeral shapes FIRST
  (a shift by a literal, a low-bits mask, a single-bit mask, `~`) since
  they cost nothing at any width and are what the tree writes. A crossing
  the encoder does not take is an `open` row an author cannot close; the
  Int forms are not a crossing at all. **Recommendation: yes, as written.**
- **S-55 — the crossing's width.** Measured (§1): free at ≤ 64 bits for
  numeral-operand shapes, 4% of the budget per row at 128, 18% at 256, the
  budget at 2048; a two-unknowns `&` under an inequality can exhaust the
  budget at 32. **Recommendation: `BV_CROSS_MAX_BITS = 64`** as a named
  constant, wider general operations opaque (safe, unproven), the Int forms
  covering the wide widths' real shapes, and the gate that no discharged row
  regresses. Moving the constant later is a measurement, not a decision.
- **S-56 — floats.** Tier 1 as QF_FP terms for `+ − * / #sqrt` and the
  comparisons (no new rows: floats never trap; the terms make `limit`,
  contract and `prove` rows over floats encodable); the manifest's tier
  column fed from the encoder (`int`/`bv`/`fp`/`real`/`-`); tier 2 as the
  Real-interval abstraction under the three soundness conditions of §2
  step 3, asked by the runner only when tier 1 answers `unknown`; `frem`
  and casts out of a float stay opaque until 1.5.8's `cast-range` rows.
  D-218 (5) is ratified; what this asks is whether tier 2's stated
  soundness conditions are the design. **Recommendation: yes.** The
  alternative — tier 2 as a later subcycle — leaves D-218 (5) half-landed
  with no float row in the tree to force the question later.
- **S-57 — `simd` lanes.** Per-lane terms (N copies of the scalar theory,
  N ≤ 16 by D-194's cap on lanes) and one row per division site over the
  lane conjunction, retiring the last `unencoded` producer but the
  struct-`limit` residue. **Recommendation: yes.** The alternative (keep
  `unencoded`) leaves P-12 open for no saving.

### Step 3 — LANDED (2026-09-10): floats in two tiers — QF_FP terms, the tier column, the Real-interval twin (D-281)
