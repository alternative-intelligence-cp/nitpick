## 1. The settled scope (D-218 items 4 and 5; S-30 → the README row, ratified 2026-09-04)

- **Integers stay unbounded `Int` with range axioms** (1.5.0); **bitwise
  operations cross into QF_BV with explicit crossing casts** — `& | ^ << >>`
  and the flag families (D-230) — so a division whose divisor was computed by
  a mask is provable, where today the term is opaque and the row `open`.
- **`tbb`/`tfp` (and `dim256` through `tfp256`) as SCALED `Int` with
  ERR-sentinel rows, never FP theory**: the carrier's value is an `Int`, the
  sentinel (`INT_MIN` of the carrier width, D-008/D-144) is a distinguished
  value every operation's hypothesis excludes or propagates, `tfp`'s Q scale a
  constant factor. 1.5.8's `err-exit` rows presuppose this encoding.
- **Floats two-tier** — D-218 (5), verbatim (read 2026-09-10): "floats
  two-tier — Z3 QF_FP for tier-1 obligations, Real-interval abstraction for
  heavy non-linear, the manifest recording which tier discharged what,
  undischarged = retained runtime guard". So tier 2 is a Real-interval
  ABSTRACTION (an over-approximation the manifest names as the discharging
  tier), not a second solver theory, and §2 step 2 is written from that text.
- Until it lands, a `limit` over such a subject and every division or
  overflow in those families is `unencoded` or absent (guard retained — safe,
  unproven). A `simd` division is `record_unencoded_division` today.

## 3. Questions to settle at planning (each with a recommendation and a measurement before it is asked; S-numbers minted then)

- The ERR-sentinel rows: a NEW obligation kind per twisted operation
  (`err-sentinel`: "this operation's result is not ERR", guard `yes` — the
  trap-on-ERR sites) or the sentinel purely as a hypothesis feeding the
  existing `div-zero`/`overflow`/`err-exit` kinds? Recommendation to be
  formed after counting the trap sites per family in `src/` (there are
  four `tbbN:Hash` impls spelling `=>!` in the prelude) — the catalogue's
  own rule (D-218.7: every carried guard appears or the manifest has holes)
  argues for a kind per GUARD, so every trap site that exists today gets a
  row.
- Which bitwise operations cross (all five and the flag families' `|`/`&`,
  or arithmetic-shaped shifts only): recommendation all, since a crossing
  the encoder does not take is an `open` row an author cannot close.
- The QF_BV width for `uint128`…`uint2048` (D-231's wide ladder): bit-vectors
  of the exact width (z3 handles 2048-bit BV; budget under `rlimit` to be
  measured on the prelude's `uint2048` Dragon4 bodies — the `ToString`
  impls are the heaviest bitwise users in the tree).
- Floats: does tier 2 apply to the compiler's own code at all (measure:
  `src/` float sites), and which float rows exist (D-007 says float
  division does not trap — confirm in `smt_kinds.npk` which kinds arm for
  floats today).
- `simd` lanes: per-lane encoding (N copies of the scalar theory) or
  `unencoded` kept for 1.5.4b — recommendation: per-lane for N ≤ 16 under
  the same scalar rules, since `record_unencoded_division` is the only
  `unencoded` producer left in the walk and P-12 wants it gone.

**The 8 open rows of `nitpick.obligations` are not bitwise cases** (from
`npkg verify --explain` on `5f13220`): three are `numeric.num_scan`'s
`div-zero` with the model setting the PARAMETER `radix` to 0 — a callee
knows nothing of its argument, so these are a `requires`/`limit` question,
not a theory's; four are `type_resolve.fold_binary`'s `div-zero`/`div-min`
pairs whose divisor is an OPAQUE term (a folded operand's value, read out of
the AST's payload — opaque by construction, no operator involved); one is
`prelude.npk_gcd256`'s `div-min` with the dividend at the 256-bit minimum
(the loop condition proves the `div-zero` half, nothing bounds the dividend).
So the QF_BV crossing closes NONE of the compiler's own open rows; its value
is for programs shaped like the `ToString` tables (a masked divisor) and the
libraries' hash code — the bitwise-shaped `open` row the skeleton's §4 asked
for does not exist in `src/` today, and the plan's `divz_masked.npk` will be
its first.

**z3 4.16.0 under the profile** (`smt.random_seed=0 sat.random_seed=0
rlimit=20000000`, `-st`; the probes are in the session's scratchpad, to be
committed as `tests/verify/` programs when the steps land):

- QF_BV at 2048 bits: `(x << 1) + x = x * 3` negated → `unsat`,
  `rlimit-count` 2,128, 0.56 s, 18 MB; a masked-divisor nonzero fact (`y =
  (x & ~1) | 2 ⇒ y ≠ 0`) at 2048 bits → `unsat` in 0.02 s. **The exact width
  is affordable; D-231's wide ladder needs no narrowing.**
- QF_FP `flt64` (11, 53): a bounded quotient (`1 ≤ a, b ≤ 2 ⇒ a / b` finite
  and not NaN) → `unsat`, `rlimit-count` 4,205,075 (21% of the budget),
  4.86 s, 322 MB peak — tier 1 decides it, at ~2,000× the bit-vector query's
  cost.
- QF_FP non-linear: bounded `a, b ⇒ sqrt(a·a + b·b)` finite and non-negative
  → **`unknown` at `rlimit-count` 20,000,041**, the budget, in 4.61 s. Exactly
  D-218 (5)'s split: the heavy non-linear shape exhausts tier 1 and is tier
  2's (the Real-interval abstraction) or a retained guard.

**The shift-amount rule (§2 step 0's "D-?"):** no checker rule and no
emitted guard for a shift amount ≥ the width was found by grep
(`ShiftRange`, "shift amount", a compare beside `shl`/`lshr` in
`emit_arith_value`) — the `shl`/`ashr` hits in `ir_expr.npk` are the `tfp`
internals with constant amounts. LLVM's `shl`/`lshr`/`ashr` by an amount ≥
the width is POISON. **To confirm by a probe before §2 is written**: if a
user `x << n` with `n ≥ width` reaches LLVM unguarded, that is a soundness
defect to fix in step 0 (a guard and a row, `shift-range`, joining the
catalogue under D-218.7's rule) before the crossing is encoded.
