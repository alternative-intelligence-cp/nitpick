## 1. Where you are

`main` is the 1.5.3 close (`b2f7d94`); the snapshot is 1.5.1b's close (D-205:
`src/`, `lib/` and `npkg/` carry no `prove`, no `assert_static`, no contract —
measured: zero of each; nothing here needs a refresh). `nitpick.obligations`
holds 178 rows (154 discharged), of which 11 `div-zero` and 10 `div-min` rows
are `open`.

**What exists.** The encoder (`src/backend/smt/smt_encode.npk`) walks one
function instance in program order over source-derived SSA versions
(`bind_define`/`bind_assign`/`invalidate`, P-5), records obligations with a
site key and a role (`record_at`, L-13), gives hypotheses DOMINANCE through
branch regions (`region_enter`/`region_leave`, `region_dominates`; the cone in

the site, P-4), havocs a loop's assigned names before and after its body
(`walk_loop`), knows a `while`/`when` condition and a loop's invariant as
hypotheses INSIDE the body (1.5.3 L-8) and the invariant after a loop nothing
`break`s (`loop_has_break`), substitutes a callee's parameters by position
(`enc_under`), and treats an address-taken name as never named (DEF-14). An
`if` encodes its condition and walks both arms in child regions, restoring the

**The counted loop as the emitter lowers it** (`emit_counted`, `ir_stmt.npk`
~1048, D-022): `start`, `limit` and `step` are evaluated ONCE at entry into
`i64`; a non-positive step traps `-4101` (`BadStep`) before the first head
visit, literal or not; the direction `asc = start < limit` is computed once and
stored; the head runs the invariant checks, then `asc ? $ < limit : $ > limit`;
the increment is `asc ? $ + step : $ - step`; `continue` re-enters at the
increment. `till(limit, step)` is `loop(0, limit, step)`. The `for` range form
(`emit_for`): `lo` and the exclusive `hi` (an inclusive `..` bound is `hi + 1`
at the range expression, `ExprRangeExpr`'s payload 1) are read from the range
value ONCE at entry (D-234), the binding starts at `lo`, the head compares
`< hi` after the invariant checks, the increment is `+ 1`. `when`
(`emit_when`): a flag set when the body first runs; after the loop, `then` if
the flag is set, else `end` (D-027 — `then` also after a `break`).

**Measured on `b2f7d94` while planning — three findings, all fixed here.**

- **DEF-26 — a guard site inside a `pick` EXPRESSION arm has no row.**
  `expr_children`'s `ExprPickExpr` arm lists only the selector ("a value-pick's
  arms are statements"), and nothing walks those statements. A probe with
  three divisions in `main` (one inside `pick (s) { (1i32) { give 100i32 / d; },
  … }`) emits three `-4097` traps and records TWO rows — P-12's rule ("every
  guard site has a row") broken since 1.5.0, invisible because no verify test
  and no function in `src/` has the shape (the belt that counts traps by group
  would have failed the verify stage on either). Not a soundness hole: the
  guard stays. The manifest is not the inventory it claims to be.
- **DEF-27 — a callee's parameter name that collides with an ADDRESS-TAKEN
  caller local binds unnamed** in `enc_under` (`bind_define` consults the
  caller's escaped set for a substitution binding that is nothing of the
  caller's), so the call-site `requires` row and the `ensures` hypothesis are
  `open`/absent. Fail-closed and weak, not unsound; 1.5.3's handoff named it.
- **DEF-28 — a non-positive LITERAL step compiles and traps at run time.**
  D-022 and CONTROL_REFERENCE §2.4: "a negative step is a compile error; so is
  a zero step". `till(3i32, 0i32) { … }` passes the checker and exits 38
  (`BadStep`). The rule was never implemented; the runtime guard is the only
  enforcement, and no obligation kind names it (§4, S-46).

### Step 1 — path conditions as hypotheses (L-1…L-6)

The rule: a branch condition is a fact inside the arm it guards, its negation a
fact where the arm cannot have been taken. Every hypothesis below is pushed
INSIDE a child region (so it dominates only that arm) unless stated otherwise.

- **`if`** (`walk_stmt`'s arm): `c = enc_expr(cond)` as today (LOUD, the parent
  region: the condition runs on every path); then-region: `c` pushed when `c`
  is a Bool term; else-region: `(not c)`. A condition outside the fragment
  pushes nothing. **After the `if`** (L-2): `stmt_falls_through` decides each
  arm; when the then-arm never falls through and the else-arm does (or is
  absent), `(not c)` is pushed in the PARENT region and the bindings keep the
  else-arm's end versions (nothing of the then-arm reaches here); the mirror
  for an else-arm that never falls through; when neither falls through nothing
  is pushed (the code after is unreachable; its rows stay whatever they are).
  When both fall through the merge is today's `invalidate` until step 2.
- **`pick` statement** (L-3): the selector's term `sel` (LOUD, the parent).
  Per arm, a CONDITION term: `PatValue` → `(= sel v)` with `v` the pattern's
  expression encoded in the parent before the arm (a literal or a constant);
  `PatRange` → `(and (<= lo sel) (< sel hi))`, `<=` when inclusive;
  `PatWildcard` → `(not (or c_1 … c_{i-1}))` when every earlier arm has a
  condition term, else none; `PatErrPattern`, struct and enum destructures →
  none; a selector outside the fragment → none for every arm. Inside arm `i`'s
  region: its condition, and `(not c_j)` for every earlier arm `j` with one
  (arms are tried in order). A `where` guard is encoded inside the arm's region
  (it runs only when the pattern matched) and pushed there as a fact; when ANY
  arm of the pick carries a guard, only each arm's own pattern condition is
  pushed — no negations of earlier arms and no wildcard condition, since the
  order of guarded arms is not a fact the patterns alone express. When the pick
  holds a `fall` anywhere, NO arm gets a condition: a target arm is entered
  from another. The pick EXPRESSION form gets the same conditions through the
  shared `walk_pick_arms`.
- **`while`** (L-4): after the loop, when `loop_has_break` denies, the
  condition is encoded QUIET over the post-havoc versions and `(not cond)` is
  pushed in the parent region after the invariant's re-push. Quiet, because the
  condition's sites were recorded once inside the body region (where they are
  evaluated at every head visit, the invariant already checked); a second LOUD
  encoding would give one site two rows.
- **`when`** (L-5): the condition encoded QUIET over the PRE-loop versions
  before `walk_loop` (`cond_pre`); the loop half as `while`, including the
  post-loop `(not cond)` when no `break` (true whether or not the body ran).
  The `then` block in a region with `cond_pre` (the body ran at least once —
  D-027's flag is exactly "the condition held at the first head visit"); the
  `end` block in a region with `(not cond_pre)` AND, for every assigned name
  the loop havoced, `(= post pre)` (the body never ran, so the value after the
  loop is the value before it). After the statement: `invalidate` of the
  names `then`/`end` assign, as today, until step 2's merge.
- **`stmt_falls_through(e, sid, fuel)`** (L-2): false for `pass`, `fail`,
  `return`, `exit`, `trap`, `break`, `continue`; a block is false when any
  statement in it is; an `if` is false when both arms are (an absent else
  falls through); a `pick` is false when every arm is, an arm holding a `fall`
  counted as falling through; everything else — loops, `when`, `defer`, a
  call statement, `#unreachable()` — falls through. Exact in the direction
  that matters: a claim of "never falls through" is only ever made of a
  syntactic terminator, because the claim's use is a hypothesis.
- **Tests** (`tests/verify/`): `path_if.npk` (`if (d != 0i32) { q = 100i32 /
  d; }` — discharged; the else arm of `if (d == 0i32) { … } else { q = 100i32
  / d; }` — discharged; `if (d == 0i32) { exit 3i32; } q = 10i32 / d;` — the
  early-exit negation, discharged; a violating `argv` run to prove the
  program's own answer is unchanged), `path_pick.npk` (a value arm and a
  wildcard: `pick (d) { (0i32) { … }, (*) { q = 100i32 / d; } }` discharged; a
  range arm; a guarded arm `(*) where (d > 0i32)` discharged; a pick with a
  `fall` — the same division `open`), `path_while.npk` (`while (d == 0i32) { d
  = 1i32; } q = 100i32 / d;` discharged; the same with a `break` in the body
  `open`), `path_when.npk` (`when (d == 0i32) { d = 5i32; } end { q = 100i32 /
  (d + 1i32); }` discharged through `end`'s two facts; a division in `then`
  that needs the post-loop negation), `path_pickexpr.npk` (the DEF-26 shape's
  arm discharged under its arm's condition). `divz_after_branch.npk` stays
  `open` at this step (its comment says which step flips it: 2).
- **The compiler's own rows move.** Every `open` `div-zero`/`div-min` row in
  `nitpick.obligations` is a candidate; the step re-records with `--record`
  and the record lists each moved row with the branch condition that proved
  it. A row that moves the other way (discharged → open) is a defect.

Full harness on prefix p0+p1.

### Step 2 — the merge: guarded re-push and the phi (L-7…L-10)

- **Guarded re-push** (L-7): a region opened for an ARM carries its condition
  term (`region_enter_cond(e, c)`; `region_enter` is the term-less form);
  `region_leave` of a region with a condition re-pushes every hypothesis
  recorded DIRECTLY in it (region == the leaving one) into the parent as
  `(=> c H)`, syms `c.syms ∪ H.syms`. A region without a condition (a loop
  body, a `defer`, a pick arm with no pattern term) re-pushes nothing. Nested
  arms compose by construction: an inner arm's hypotheses reach the outer
  arm's region as `(=> c2 H)` and the outer's parent as `(=> c1 (=> c2 H))`.
  Soundness is L-7's argument in §3.
- **The phi after `if`, `when` and `pick`** (L-8): with `snap0` the bindings
  before the construct and one end-snapshot per arm that falls through, every
  binding of `snap0`'s range whose versions differ between the falling-through
  arms gets a NEW version defined `(ite c_1 v_1 (ite c_2 v_2 … v_n))` — for an
  `if`, `(ite c then else)` — when every contributing arm has a condition term
  and every contributing version is named; else today's havoc (`invalidate`).
  An arm that never falls through contributes nothing. A limited name gets
  `assert_rule` after its merged version (L-7 of 1.5.2, unchanged). For a
  `pick`: only when no arm holds a `fall` and every arm has a condition term;
  the last arm needs none (the chain's default: the pick is exhaustive).
  `restore(snap0)` between arms stays; the end-snapshots are taken before it.
- **The pick expression's VALUE** (L-9): under the same conditions, when every
  falling-through arm's body ENDS in `give v` with `v` encodable, the
  expression's term is the `ite` chain over the arms' give terms; otherwise
  opaque as today.
- **Expression-level arms** (L-10): `a && b` encodes `b` in a child region
  with condition `a`; `a || b` with `(not a)`; the ternary's arms with `c` and
  `(not c)`; each left with the guarded re-push, so `b`'s opaque symbols keep
  their range axioms (guarded) in the parent — without the re-push these
  regions would have LOST facts the current parent-region encoding has, which
  is why they wait for this step. The term shapes are unchanged.
- **Measured before the harness** (the 1.5.2d rule): the compiler's own
  `--obligations` run — wall time, hypothesis count and cone sizes, `rows.txt`
  unchanged in row COUNT — before and after this step; `tests/cost/self.toml`'s
  ceiling holds or the record says by how much the encoder's state grew and
  why. If the guarded copies make a function's file pathological (a hypothesis
  count that grows with nesting depth is the known shape), the mitigation is a
  named constant — the maximum nesting depth re-pushed — recorded here with
  the measurement that forced it; it changes which hypotheses exist, never
  the verdict's determinism.
- **Tests**: `divz_after_branch.npk` flips to `div-zero discharged 1` (the
  1.5.0 promise; its comment rewritten to say so), `path_merge.npk` (both arms
  assign a nonzero divisor → discharged; only the then-arm assigns and the
  declaration's initialiser is zero → `open`, and the run without arguments
  traps 36 — the counterexample is real), `path_expr.npk` (`d != 0i32 &&
  100i32 / d > 1i32`, `d == 0i32 || 100i32 / d > 1i32`, `is (d == 0i32) : 0i32
  : 100i32 / d` — all discharged), `path_pick_value.npk` (a pick expression's
  value through the chain: `int32:k = pick (s) { (1i32) { give 5i32; }, (*) {
  give 7i32; } }; q = 100i32 / k;` discharged), `path_nested.npk` (two nested
  ifs, the inner fact reaching a site after both). The compiler's own rows
  move again; recorded the same way.

Full harness on prefix p0+p1+p2.

### Step 3 — the counters: `$`, the `for` binding, the step (L-11…L-14)

- **`$` has a term** (L-11; 1.5.2 L-19 closes). In the counted arm of
  `walk_stmt`: `start` (`0` for `till`), `limit` and `step` encoded LOUD in
  the parent (their sites) and CAPTURED — `term_capture(e, ty, t)`: a fresh
  anonymous symbol defined equal to the term, so a body that assigns a name
  the bound mentions cannot move it (D-234's rule, and what the emitter's
  slots do); `asc = (< start limit)`. `Encoder.dollars` is a stack of `$`
  terms (nested counted loops; the innermost wins; `e.subject` still wins
  inside a rule). The ENTRY invariant row is recorded with `$ ↦ start`. Inside
  the body region `$` is a fresh symbol `|$.k|` of the counter's type
  (`int64`, the checker's) with the hypotheses `(=> asc (and (<= start $) (<
  $ limit)))`, `(=> (not asc) (and (< limit $) (<= $ start)))` and, when the
  step is a numeral `s`, the congruence `(= (mod (- $ start) s) 0)` ascending
  and `(= (mod (- start $) s) 0)` descending — linear, since `mod` by a
  numeral is (P-16's logic choice is unchanged). The PRESERVATION row and every
  `continue` row are recorded with `$ ↦ (ite asc (+ $ step) (- $ step))`: the
  head sees the incremented counter. After the loop (no `break`) the invariant
  is re-pushed over `$_exit`, a fresh symbol with `(=> asc (and (>= $_exit
  limit) (>= $_exit start)))`, `(=> (not asc) (and (<= $_exit limit) (<=
  $_exit start)))`, and for a numeral step `(=> asc (< $_exit (+ limit s)))`,
  `(=> (not asc) (> $_exit (- limit s)))` with the congruence — which pins
  `$_exit` exactly for a step of 1 and to one residue class otherwise. Zero
  iterations (`start == limit`, or `till` with a non-positive limit) satisfy
  every one of these with `$_exit = start`.
- **The `for` binding has a term** (L-12). Range form: `lo` and `hi` are the
  range node's two operands (`expr_lhs`/`expr_rhs`; `+ 1` on `hi` when the
  node's payload says inclusive), encoded LOUD in the parent and captured; the
  binding gets an ENTRY version `|i.1| = lo` before the loop, is listed among
  the loop's ASSIGNED names (so `walk_loop` havocs it: a fresh `|i.2|` for the
  body with `(and (<= lo i) (< i hi))` pushed inside the body region, and
  another after the loop — released with the scope); the preservation and
  `continue` rows are recorded with `i ↦ (+ i 1)`. A slice, array or
  `Iterator` form: the binding opaque, havoced the same way (nothing is known
  of an element).
- **The step's row and its guard** (L-13; §4 S-46 as recommended). A LITERAL
  step is the checker's (DEF-28): positive by TYPE-068, so the emitter emits
  NO compare and NO trap for it — and the encoder records no row (nothing to
  discharge). A COMPUTED step keeps the guard and gets a row: kind `loop-step`
  (goal `(> step 0)`, guard `yes`, SITE_STMT the loop, ROLE_GUARD, group the
  loop's statement index, traps 1), elided as the compare's removal with no
  `llvm.assume` (L-11 of 1.5.3: a contract-shaped guard's elision is its
  removal; ASSUME_KINDS unchanged). `smt_kinds.npk` gains `OK_LOOP_STEP` (18)
  and `ok_name` `"loop-step"`, `ok_has_guard` true; the catalogue table gains
  its row; both runners' `TRAP_OF_KIND`/`trap_of_kind` map it to `-4101`;
  `Z3_KINDS` extends. If S-46 is ruled the other way, the guard stays and no
  row exists — and P-12 keeps a stated hole.
- **Tests**: `inv_counted.npk` keeps `invariant open 2` (its rows are open on
  the merits — `k` from `argv` is unconstrained — and its comment says so
  instead of "opaque"), `counted_exit.npk` (`loop (0i64, 5i64, 1i64) invariant
  acc == $ * 2i64 { acc = acc + 2i64; }` then `100i64 / (acc - 9i64)`: entry,
  preservation and the post-loop `div-zero` all discharged through `$_exit ==
  5`), `counted_desc.npk` (a descending loop with a step of 3: the invariant
  over the residue class), `counted_nested.npk` (`$` of the inner loop
  inside the outer's body), `for_bounds.npk` (`for (int32:k in 1i32...4i32) {
  q = 100i32 / k; }` discharged; the inclusive `0i32..4i32` form `open`, the
  run trapping 36), `loop_step.npk` (a step computed from `argv`: `loop-step
  open 1`, the guard retained, the run without arguments exiting 38; a literal
  step in the same file: no row, no trap — `ll_is` on the IR). `inv_for.npk`'s
  counts are unchanged (its invariant never needed the binding).

Full harness on prefix p0+p1+p2+p3.

### Step 4 — `prove`, `assert_static`, `exhaustive`; the `checker` verdict; the rung suite (L-15…L-22)

- **`prove(e)` is a row and nothing else in the plain build** (L-15; §4 S-45
  as recommended). Emitter: the `StmtProveStmt` arm lowers to nothing (the
  rung retires; `KIND_STATUS` reads `lowered`). Encoder (`walk_stmt`): kind
  `prove`, SITE_STMT the statement, ROLE_GUARD, group the statement, traps 0,
  the goal `e` encoded QUIET (nothing executes, so a division inside is not a
  site; an inexpressible `e` → `unencoded`); then — S-48 as recommended —
  `e` pushed as a HYPOTHESIS after the row (L-7's order: never in its own
  cone), so a proven claim is knowledge for every site after it in its region.
- **The verified build refuses an unproven `prove`** (L-16). `--elide` parses
  the manifest into `ObTable.man_hashes`/`man_verdicts` (every row, not only
  the discharged — `man_discharged` stays for `enc_mark_discharged`) and sets
  `ObTable.elide_given`. In the emitter's `StmtProveStmt` arm: when
  `elide_given` and `llctx_elided(SITE_STMT, sid, OK_PROVE)` is false, the arm
  returns an `IrVal` in a new state `LL_VERIFY` whose `what` carries the
  sentence; `emit_program.npk`'s `refuse` maps `LL_VERIFY` to
  `NITPICK-VERIFY-001` (`VERIFY_UNPROVEN`, `emit_codes.npk`): "this `prove` is
  not discharged by the manifest the verified build was given (`open`: the
  solver found a path where it can be false; `budget`; `unencoded`: outside
  the encoder's fragment; or absent from the manifest) — a verified build
  refuses an unproven claim (VERIFICATION §1.2); `npkg verify --explain`
  writes the model". The word comes from `man_verdicts` by the row's hash.
  Under a plain build (`--obligations` alone, or neither flag) nothing is
  refused: the plain build elides nothing and claims nothing.
- **`assert_static(e)` folds at the frontend** (L-17). `type_stmt.npk`'s arm:
  after `check_proposition`, `fold_const(e.tr, expr, scope)` (0.6's evaluator,
  the `comptime(...)` arm's path); a value that is not a folded `bool` →
  `NITPICK-TYPE-069` (`TYPE_ASSERT_STATIC`): "an `assert_static` proposition

## 3. Decisions taken at planning

- **L-1 — a condition is a hypothesis in the arm it guards, and only there.**
  The region tree already gives hypotheses dominance; a branch condition
  pushed inside the arm's region is exactly VERIFICATION §1.2's "branch guards
  … accumulated and asserted as axioms". Nothing is pushed for a condition
  outside the fragment (opaque: no fact).
- **L-2 — an arm that never falls through gives its negation to what
  follows**, and the "never falls through" claim is made only of a syntactic
  terminator (`pass`, `fail`, `return`, `exit`, `trap`, `break`, `continue`,
  and blocks, `if`s and `pick`s made only of those). A claim in the other
  direction costs precision; a claim in this direction wrongly made would be a
  false hypothesis, so nothing else qualifies — not a `while (true)`, not
  `#unreachable()`, not a `fall`.
- **L-3 — a `pick` arm's condition is its pattern's, plus the negations of
  the arms before it; a guard anywhere in the pick keeps only each arm's own
  pattern; a `fall` anywhere keeps nothing.** A guarded arm's failure is not
  expressible from the pattern alone, and a `fall` enters an arm without its
  pattern.
- **L-4 — after a loop nothing `break`s, its condition is false**, encoded
  QUIET over the post-havoc versions so one site keeps one row.
- **L-5 — `when`'s `then` and `end` are the two arms of "did the body run",
  which is the condition's value at the first head visit.** In `end` the
  post-loop versions equal the pre-loop ones, a fact D-027's own table
  states.
- **L-6 — the order inside a body region is: the invariant, the condition,
  then the body**, unchanged from 1.5.3; the new hypotheses of steps 1 and 3
  join at the same point.
- **L-7 — a hypothesis of an arm is a fact after the arm, guarded by the
  arm's condition.** A hypothesis recorded directly in an arm's region is a
  fact of every execution that reaches its site; an execution that reaches
  the point after the arm with the condition true fell through the arm's
  straight line and so passed every site of it (an early exit directly in
  the line means the arm never falls through, and a trap ends the
  execution); when the condition is false the implication is vacuous. A
  nested arm's hypotheses arrive already guarded by the inner condition. A
  LOOP BODY's hypotheses are one symbolic iteration's and are NOT re-pushed
  (after the loop only the invariant and the negated condition speak); a
  `defer` body's are not either. This is what makes the phi sound: the
  merged version names an arm's version, and the arm's definition of it is
  available exactly under that arm's condition. The 1.5.0 cone comment's
  hazard — a then-arm's in-range definition telling an else-arm site that
  `x.1 < MAX` — cannot arise: the else-arm has `(not c)` and the guarded copy
  `(=> c …)` is vacuous there.
- **L-8 — the merge is an `ite` over the arms' end versions, and a havoc
  where any contributing arm has no condition term or an unnamed version.**
  One shape for `if`, `when` and `pick`; an arm that never falls through
  contributes nothing.
- **L-9 — a pick expression's value is the `ite` chain of its arms' `give`
  terms under the same conditions, else opaque.**
- **L-10 — `&&`, `||` and the ternary are arms too**, and they wait for the
  guarded re-push (step 2) because a child region without it would LOSE the
  range axioms the parent-region encoding has today.
- **L-11 — `$` is a term: the entry value at the entry row, a fresh in-range
  symbol in the body, the incremented value at the preservation and
  `continue` rows, and an exit value after the loop**, with the direction a
  term (`asc = start < limit`) as the emitter computes it once, and the
  bounds captured as the emitter's slots are (D-234's rule for `for`). The
  congruence rides only for a numeral step: `mod` by a numeral is linear, by
  a symbol it is not.
- **L-12 — the `for` binding is an assigned name of its loop**: an entry
  version equal to `lo`, a havoced body version in `[lo, hi)`, the
  preservation rows over `i + 1`. Only the range form knows anything.
- **L-13 — a literal step is the checker's, a computed step is a row with a
  guard.** The emitter emits no compare for a literal (TYPE-068 made it
  positive), and a computed step's guard is the `loop-step` row's (S-46). No
  `llvm.assume` at elision (1.5.3 L-11's rule).
- **L-14 — `$`'s type is the checker's `int64`**, and the bounds' terms are
  plain Ints whatever the literals' widths (the emitter widens to `i64` the
  same way).
- **L-15 — `prove` lowers to nothing and records a guard-less row; its
  operand is walked QUIET.** The catalogue ratified guard `no`; nothing
  executes, so a division inside the proposition is not a site (SMT's total
  `div` makes the proof sound whatever value it takes).
- **L-16 — the VERIFIED build refuses an undischarged `prove` by name**
  (S-45), in the compiler under `--elide`, because the compiler has the span
  and P-27 says it trusts the manifest it is handed: a manifest that does not
  discharge a claim is a manifest under which the claim is unproven. The
  refusal is a diagnostic with its own code (`VERIFY-001`), tested like every
  code (`check_codes_tested`), and the `IrVal` state that carries it is the
  third beside `LL_RUNG` and `LL_BROKEN`.
- **L-17 — `assert_static` is the frontend's, through 0.6's evaluator**, one
  code for its two failures, silent when the evaluator already spoke.
- **L-18 — in a `comptime` body both statements are evaluated.** A false
  `prove` on a concrete evaluation is a counterexample; refusing it is the
  strictest reading and the consistent one ("the compiler verifies it" — by
  evaluation, where it evaluates).
- **L-19 — every `pick` is an `exhaustive` row with verdict `checker`**, both
  forms, because the catalogue says so and the 0.5 analysis is the
  discharger. No `pick` reaches the encoder uncovered.
- **L-20 — `checker` is a third state of a row, not a verdict z3 gave**: a
  `c` in `rows.txt`, no `(check-sat)`, tier `-`, word `none`. Both runners
  read it, `parity` holds them equal.
- **L-21 — a verify test's `expect-error` means the VERIFIED build refuses**,
  after the rows are decided and compared: one file shows the whole path —
  the rows, the verdict, the refusal — and D-237's exactness applies.
- **L-22 — the rung suite retires when the last rung falls**, by decision
  (S-47), with its one test kept as a positive program; the parser-never-
  restricts rule is the grammar sweep's, and the check that a rung names an
  open row stays for the day one returns.
- **L-23 — no snapshot refresh, no adoption.** `src/` carries none of these
  constructs; every change is under the builder as it stands (D-205).
- **L-24 — every step re-records `nitpick.obligations` when it moves, and the
  record explains each moved row.** A row that moves from `discharged` to
  `open` is a defect of the step, never a re-baseline (D-040, P-9).

## 4. Questions for the user (the plan proceeds under the recommendations)

- **S-45 — an undischarged `prove` refuses the VERIFIED build, and the plain
  build lowers `prove` to nothing.** VERIFICATION §1.2 and CONTROL §4.4: "if
  the solver finds a counterexample, compilation fails"; D-218.7 ratified
  `prove` with guard `no`; C-14/D-219: "an undischarged obligation RETAINS its
  runtime guard — the binary differs only with the manifest saying so" — and a
  `prove` has no guard to retain, so the only honest outcome for a verified
  artifact with an unproven claim in it is to refuse. **Recommendation:**
  `npkc --elide` refuses an undischarged `prove` (`open`, `budget`,
  `unencoded`, or absent from the manifest) with `NITPICK-VERIFY-001` at the
  statement; `npkg verify` fails through it; the plain build is untouched (no
  check, no trap: guard `no` as ratified). The alternative gives `prove` a
  runtime guard in every build (an amendment to D-218.7's column, a new trap
  identity, and a `prove` that behaves like an `ensures`), which makes
  `prove` a second spelling of a check the language already has.
  **Measured:** zero `prove` in `src/`, `lib/`, `npkg/`; seven test files
  mention it (grammar, parser and checker fixtures, the accept file, the rung
  file).
- **S-46 — the counted loop's step guard becomes a catalogue kind,
  `loop-step`.** D-022: a non-literal step is "a proof obligation, falling
  back to a runtime check that traps to `failsafe`"; the runtime check
  exists (`-4101`, `BadStep`, at every counted loop's entry) and no kind
  names it, so the manifest is not the inventory of guards P-12 says it is.
  **Recommendation:** add the kind — goal `step > 0`, guard `yes`, rows from
  1.5.4, trap `-4101` — an amendment to D-218.7's list (the list was ratified
  as "every carried obligation appears or the manifest has holes"; this is a
  hole the catalogue's own rule asks to close). The alternative leaves the
  guard row-less and states the hole in §7b. **Measured:** three counted
  loops in `src/` (all literal steps), `emit_counted` traps for every step
  today, DEF-28 (a literal `0i32` step compiles and exits 38).
- **S-47 — the rung suite retires.** After `prove` and `assert_static`
  lower, `ll_rung` has no caller and no construct in the language rungs
  (measured: every remaining `iv_rung`/`pv_rung` is reached only through an
  `LlType` state nothing constructs). `tests/rejection/`'s README says the
  directory shrinking measures subset 1 disappearing; this is the last step
  of that measurement. **Recommendation:** retire the directory and its
  `[[test]]` entry, keep `inline_mod.npk` as a positive program (its
  original hole — declared code silently shed — is caught by a wrong answer),
  keep `check_rung_names_open_cycle` for a rung a later cycle adds, and
  re-home D-085's rule to BUILD_REFERENCE §7.1. The alternative keeps an
  empty suite both runners must special-case, asserting nothing.
- **S-48 — a discharged `prove` is knowledge after its site (a lemma).**
  VERIFICATION §1.2 says the solver "constructs a mathematical proof that the
  expression holds", and a proven proposition is a fact of every execution
  that passes the statement. **Recommendation:** push the proposition as a
  hypothesis after its row, so `prove` states an intermediate fact later
  obligations use — a proof outline in source, which is what the safety
  story wants authors writing. Sound because a verified build refuses an
  undischarged `prove` (S-45) and a plain build elides nothing; a later row
  discharged through an unproven `prove` ships in no artifact. The
  alternative keeps `prove` a documentation-only check whose fact the
  encoder forgets one line later.
