## 3. Decisions taken at planning

- **L-1 — The rule lives in one predicate.** `type_owns_for_move` is the one
  place "does this type own, for the move-only rule" is answered; the three
  sites that asked `type_drops` directly ask it.
- **L-2 — The generator refuses what the checker would refuse.** A generated
  lending `pick` over a `T` payload is the same copy; refusing it at the derive
  (DERIVE-006, D-259's re-homing) is the honest report. The derives that bind
  nothing keep generating.
- **L-3 — Seven respellings, no rewrites.** Each site stores a by-value `T:v`
  into an owning slot; `move T:v` and `move(v)` is the D-183 spelling for a
  concrete owning type and the same for a `T`.
