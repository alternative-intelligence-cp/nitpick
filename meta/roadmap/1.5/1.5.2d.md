## 2. What this subcycle builds, in one picture

Two things, both semantics-neutral. **The frontend's per-program cost becomes
bounded by the program**: the bindings analysis (`src/frontend/analysis/
bindings.npk`) sizes its state by the FUNCTION's locals and parameters instead
of by every statement and declaration in the whole program; the type table
(`types.npk`, `tt_intern`) and the string interner (`intern.npk`,
`intern_add`) dedupe through a hash index instead of a linear scan. **The
emitted IR contains no unreferenced prelude item**: each non-generic prelude
function and each prelude impl's vtable is bracketed as an item of the module
text, and at the close of emission an item is kept only if a symbol it defines
is referenced from outside every item or from a kept item — a fixpoint over the
text itself. Measured before and after on the probe and on the compiler's own
build; every existing test reports exactly what it did.

## 3. Decisions taken at planning (each with its reason)

- **L-1 — The frontend fixes are engineering under D-262 §2, not design.** A
  verdict-neutral change to how an analysis stores its state, or how a table
  finds an entry, changes no rule; the harness's every-test-identical run and
  the compiler's byte-identical IR are the proof. They come FIRST (step 1)
  because they dominate the measurement (§4: 57% + 13% + the interner's share)
  and because the emission change is measured against a frontend that no longer
  hides it.
- **L-2 — The bindings analysis numbers a function's slots at RESOLUTION.**
  The resolver declares every local (`resolve.npk:352`, the one `SYM_STMT`
  declaration site) and every parameter (`resolve_decl`'s `DeclFunctionDecl`
  arm) with the function's scope open; a counter on the `Resolver` reset at
  each function's entry gives each parameter then each local a dense ordinal,
  recorded in the symbol table (`symtab_set_local_ord`, a sparse map by symbol
  id exactly as `decl_home` is by declaration), and the function's total on
  its declaration (`symtab_set_fn_slots`, by DeclId). `symbol_slot` returns
  that ordinal for a `SYM_STMT` symbol and for a `DeclParamDecl` symbol — the
  two kinds it tracks today; everything else stays −1 — and `state_new` is
  sized by the function's total. Why at resolution and not by a walk in the
  analysis: a walk over the body's statement kinds is complete only if it
  names every kind that nests a block, and a kind it misses is a local the
  analysis silently stops tracking (fails OPEN); a walk over the symbol
  table's scopes is complete but linear in the PROGRAM per function, the very
  shape being removed. The resolver sees every declaration by construction,
  after expansion and derive synthesis have spliced their text.
- **L-3 — Hash indexes over the two tables, keyed exactly as the scans key.**
  `tt_intern` keys on `(kind, a, b, c, name)` and deliberately not on size
  (the comment at `types.npk:311`); the index keys the same, open addressing
  in a `List<int32>` of power-of-two capacity with −1 empty, rebuilt at half
  load, hashed through the tree's own `fnv1a_step` (`intern.npk`, the D-179
  seed — no new hash, no overflow: the step computes wide and narrows).
  `intern_add` keys on the text (`fnv1a_bytes`) with `string_eq` on the
  probe. Every push into either table goes through the one function that
  indexes it (`types.npk:302`, `intern.npk:106`; `types.npk:1460/1471` push
  into `unit_vecs`/`unit_decls`, a different list).
- **L-4 — Trimming is decided over the emitted text, never by an AST walk
  (D-262 §1).** An item is a range of one sink recorded by the writer
  (`irw_item_begin`/`irw_item_end`: the current sink's identity and length at
  begin, the length at end; an item whose sink changed in between is
  discarded, i.e. kept as root). The emitter brackets, in `emit_all`'s module
  loop for `m == g.prelude` only: each `emit_function` of a non-generic
  function, and inside `emit_impl_block` each method and each `emit_vtable`
  (the vtable global with its thunks). Generic instances (`emit_fn_instance`,
  `emit_family_instance`), drop bodies, vacant bodies, headers, declares,
  globals, the string table and the site tables are never items: they are
  roots. A symbol reference is an `@` token — `@"…"` quoted or bare
  `@[A-Za-z0-9_.$]+` — anywhere in the text; a definition is the token after
  `define …` on a line beginning `define`, or the token beginning a line
  `@name = …`. The fixpoint: root references seed a worklist; a referenced
  symbol's defining item is kept and its own references enqueued; the sinks
  are rebuilt from their non-item bytes and kept items in original order.
  Fails closed: a token no rule anticipated is a reference that keeps its
  item; a string constant mentioning a name without `@` is not a reference,
  which is right. Cost: linear in the text.
- **L-5 — A belt in both runners.** `check_prelude_trimmed`: in the compiler's
  own emitted IR (the harness already holds it for `selfhost`), every `define`
  whose symbol begins `@"npk.prelude.` is referenced somewhere else in the
  file; a define nobody references is a FAILURE naming it — the trim did not
  run, or a new emission path bypassed the brackets. `npkg` performs the same
  check on `build/npkc.ll` in `npkg build`. Also a unit: `tests/backend/
  programs/prelude_trim.npk` (uses `int_to_string` and nothing else of the
  prelude; `// ll:` markers assert the used symbol's define is present and a
  never-used one — `npk.prelude.int8:ToString.to_string` — is absent).
- **L-6 — What must not move.** `nitpick.obligations` (141 rows: the encoder
  walks the AST, emission is downstream); every existing test's verdict; the
  compiler's own IR byte-identical after step 1 (frontend only) and changed
  after step 2 only by the dropped items (measured: the kept text is a
  subsequence of the old text); `parity` and the two byte-identity assertions.
- **L-7 — Order.** Step 1 the frontend (measured per fix on the probe and on
  `src/npkc.npk`), step 2 the trim (measured the same way plus llc time),
  step 3 the docs and the notice to the workbench (`nitpick-libs_s1`, or
  whoever `BOARD.md` names as writer) with the canary's after-value.

  including `intern_add`'s scan); `type_stmt.check_module` 15.9%;
  `tt_intern` 13.5%.
- **The state's size**: `assign_decl` calls `state_new(ast_stmt_count +
  ast_decl_count)` for EVERY function (`bindings.npk:469`) — seven `bool`
  arrays of the whole program's statement-plus-declaration count, copied at
  every branch, merge and loop (`state_copy`, `state_overwrite`); slots are
  statement indices and `stmt_count + decl index` (`symbol_slot`,
  `bindings.npk:1631`). `symbol_slot`/`binding_slot` have no caller outside
  the file.
- **The two scans**: `tt_intern` (`types.npk:276`) walks every type comparing
  five fields; `intern_add` (`intern.npk:97`) walks every interned string with
  `string_eq`.
- **The writer**: the whole module is buffered (`irw_to_buffer`; `out` is the
  head, `types` the frame types, `tail` everything after the first function —
  `irw_sink` switches to `tail` once `seen_fn`); a function's `define` line and
  its spliced body land in the same sink (`irw_close_fn`). `irw_text` is the
  assembled view; `emit_all` ends with `strtab_write_all` and the site tables.
  `g.prelude` is the prelude's index in the module graph (`graph_load_prelude`).

## 6. What the executor should know (gotchas, carried and found)

- A full harness is about 75 minutes alone (three concurrent ones ran ~3 h);
  stdout is block-buffered. `cd` EXPLICITLY at the start of every command.
- `quickcheck` rebuilds the checker each run (~1.5 min) and its 60-second
  subprocess limit is too short for `src/npkc.npk` — run the built
  `.internal/quickcheck/check` binary directly on it. `quickemit` rebuilds
  `npkc` when `src/` changed (~4 min).
- A probe's `failsafe` names `HeapOom`, `HeapBadRequest`, `Unreachable`,
  `WildLeak` (and the arithmetic three where arithmetic appears), or REACH-001
  stops it.
- Reserved words that read like locals: `any`, `on`, `in`, `mod`, `fd`,
  `fails`, `end`, `unit`, `error`, `buffer`, `raw`, `move`, `old`, `pure`,
  `result`, `limit`, `trit`/`nit`, the flag families.
- Strings are move-only owners (TYPE-046); `string_eq` is a compiler-module
  function, `string_equals` the builtin.
- D-210: plain-int overflow traps; hash arithmetic goes through `fnv1a_step`
  (wide, then narrowed) — never `h * P` on an `int64`.
- `perf` cannot open events here; `valgrind --tool=callgrind` works (30 s on
  the probe); `callgrind_annotate --inclusive=yes` reads it.
- The workbench's canary is `probe11d_floor_only.npk`, 845,282 bytes; it will
  move at step 2's landing. Its orchestrator's address changes; `ListAgents`
  is the address book.
- The user's client flags security-attack vocabulary and the column-trimming
  shell command spelled c-u-t; keep both out of prose and commands.
- Stop every background waiter before reporting done.

### Step 1 — LANDED (2026-09-05): the frontend's per-program cost bounded by the program (L-1, L-2, L-3)

**As planned.** `SymbolTable` carries three sparse maps in `decl_home`'s
shape: `local_ord` (by symbol id: a parameter's or a local's dense ordinal
within its function, −1 for a symbol that has none), `stmt_ord` (the same
ordinal by the local's DECLARING statement — added at implementation for the
two analysis sites that hold the statement and not the symbol: the
declaration's own marking, `bindings.npk`'s `StmtVarDeclStmt` arm, and the
`fixed` twice-assigned check in `assign_assign`, both of which had used the
statement index as the slot directly and were the first thing the rebuilt
checker refused, every binding in the prelude "not assigned on every path"),
and `fn_slots` (by DeclId: the function's total). `Resolver.local_next` is
reset at a function's entry, saved and restored around it, handed to each
parameter then each local as `resolve_decl` and `resolve_stmt` declare them
(the one `SYM_STMT` site, `resolve.npk:352`), and recorded as the total after
the body. `state_new` takes `symtab_fn_slots(decl)`; parameters are marked by
index, since they are numbered first; `symbol_slot` takes the symbol id and
answers the ordinal for a local and a parameter, −1 for everything else, as
before. `TypeTable.index` and `InternTable.index` are open-addressing tables
of ids under a power-of-two mask, −1 empty, rebuilt at half load, hashed
through `fnv1a_step`/`fnv1a_bytes`; `tt_intern` and `intern_add` probe once
where they scanned every entry. No List `clear` exists; an `int32` list is
truncated by its count.
