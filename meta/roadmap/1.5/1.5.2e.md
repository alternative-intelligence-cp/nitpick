## 3. Decisions taken at planning

- **L-1 — One builtin, not a special case.** The emitter could have recognised
  `list_init`'s `alloc` and lowered it differently; that is a context-dependent
  meaning of `alloc`, the blueprint violation. A NAMED builtin says what it does
  and the reference says who may call it.
- **L-2 — Prelude-only is a table fact.** The marker lives in the row and is
  generated into a predicate, as `Pure` and `Views` are; the checker reads the
  predicate, never a name list of its own.
- **L-3 — `ralloc` is not touched.** Measured: the floor's move paths allocate
  with the old block's role. A `ralloc_managed` would be a second spelling of
  a fact the floor already keeps.
- **L-4 — What must not move.** `nitpick.obligations` (141 rows; the prelude's
  List bodies gain no division), every existing verdict, the compiler's own IR
  except the `list_init`/`list_reserve` instances' one call symbol.
