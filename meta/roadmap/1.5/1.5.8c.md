## 0. What 1.5.8c is

D-218 (7) ratified termination among the catalogue's kinds; VERIFICATION_REFERENCE §7b assigns `terminate`
and `stack-depth` to this subcycle, the two kinds with no row anywhere. D-304 gave termination a surface — a
loop says why it ends (`decreases E`, a whole number that shrinks on every trip and never goes below zero) or
says that it may not (`unbounded`, the explicit opt-out), and every `while` and `when` says one of the two —
and made the measure a CHECK in every build (`DecreasesViolated`, 4119) that the `terminate` rows prove away.
The safety reason is Nikola's: a loop that never ends is a hang, and a hang reaches no `failsafe`.

## 1. Where it starts (measured 2026-09-23 on 1.5.8b's 6d tree)

- **The loops.** 851 `while`/`when` loops the census finds by their head line (D-304 counted 925 at 1.5.8's
  planning on a different pattern; the heads a regex cannot see — a condition spanning lines — are found by the
  PARSER in step 3's tool, which walks the AST): `src/` 517, `npkg/` 167, `tests/` 143, `lib/` 22, `tools/` 2;
  8 are `when`. By the shape of the condition and of the writes to its variables in the body:
  - **490** — `while (v < bound)` (or `<=`) with `v = v + 1` (or `+= 1`) in the body and no other write to
    `v`: the measure is `bound - v`, DERIVABLE, provided the bound's own variables are not written in the body
    (the tool checks that; `while (i < l.count)` with a `list_push(@l, …)` inside is NOT derivable — its
    measure grows — and goes to the reading list). Examples: `src/npkc.npk:84 while (ai < argv.len)`,
    `src/prelude/prelude.npk:900 while (i < self.len)`.
  - **60** — the descending twin, `while (v > bound)` with `v - 1`: `v - bound`.
  - **62** — `while (true)` with a `break`: a measure exists in most (the lexer's scans over `pos < len`,
    a retry loop over a budget) and must be READ; a few are event loops (`unbounded`).
  - **16** — `while (true)` with no `break`: the executor's run loop, the reactor's wait, a driver's
    dispatch — `unbounded` candidates, each read.
  - **98** compound conditions, **84** loops whose variable is written but not by ±1 (`n = n / 2`,
    `R = R - S`, a `list_pop`), **25** flag or call conditions (`while (go)`, `while (raw next(@it))`),
    **10** whose condition variable is written by nothing in the body (a call moves it, a pointer):
    ~217 loops READ one by one, each given its measure or `unbounded` with a one-line reason.
  So: roughly 550 loops written by a tool, 300 by a reader; every one of them runs under the harness, and a
  wrong measure TRAPS (`DecreasesViolated`) the first time the loop runs — the sweep is self-checking to the
  extent the loops are exercised, and the compiler's own 517 run on every build of `src/`.
- **The keywords.** `decreases` and `unbounded` as CODE identifiers (comments and strings excluded): one in
  the tree — `tests/types/rejection/generics.npk:20`'s `func:unbounded<U>` — and none in `nitpick-libs` or
  `nitpick-apps` (measured over both trees). The floor spec's grammar refuses `(decreases a)` as an unknown loop
  sub-clause (`npkg/selfcheck.npk:1199`); that is a string, untouched.
- **The codes.** `DecreasesViolated = 4119` is DECLARED in the prelude since 1.5.8's planning and armed by
  nothing; `NITPICK-TYPE-072`…`075` are reserved by D-304 and defined nowhere.
- **The precedents the mechanism mirrors, each read:**
  - the clause: `p_parse_invariant` after a `while`'s condition (`parse_stmt.npk:703`), a `VerifyInvariantNode`
    in the statement's header slot; `stmt_while_invariant`/`stmt_when_invariant` accessors; typed at
    `type_stmt.npk:968` under TYPE-060's admission (a proposition evaluates anywhere: no `await`, `move`, store,
    call but of a `pure never fails` function);
  - the check: `emit_invariant_checks` (`ir_rules.npk:575`) — one `emit_contract_check` per clause under the
    loop's clause context (`x.fe.ctx.cur_ctx = sid`), elided only when EVERY row of the loop is discharged
    (`invariant_elided`: the entry row at the statement, the preservation row at the body block, one per
    `continue` re-entering the loop — DEF-81);
  - the rows: `walk_loop` (`smt_encode.npk:3716`) — the entry row at `SITE_STMT` the statement, the invariant
    a hypothesis inside the body's region with the condition, the preservation row at the body block, one per
    `continue`, the invariant a hypothesis after a loop nothing `break`s;
  - the function-side predicate: `emit_checked_entry` (`ir_rules.npk:265`) — a split function's checked entry
    calls `<sym>.req` over its parameter registers and tail-calls `.body` (D-252, D-268);
  - the arm: `reach_invariant` (`reach.npk:462`) sets `saw_invariant` where a loop carries the clause and walks
    the clauses for their own arms; `reach_callee` (DEF-86) resolves every call's declaration, which the
    recursive-group pass reuses;
  - the frame: a coroutine's loop spans suspensions, so a per-loop "previous measure" slot is a FRAME slot,
    reserved by the pre-scan as role 41's join marks are (`ir_stmt.npk:500`); roles in use are 3, 7, 30, 41 and
    `60+k` (`old(...)` snapshots).
- **The recursive groups.** 103 groups of 285 functions in the compiler's own emission at 1.5.8's planning
  (direct calls of `build/npkc.ll`). D-304 §5 keeps the function measure OPTIONAL; nothing in the tree writes
  one, and the tree's own recursion is not swept.

## 2. The design

### 2.1 The surface (step 1; D-304 §§1, 4)

- **Two keywords**, `decreases` and `unbounded`, in LEXICAL_REFERENCE §4's VerificationKeyword production
  (beside `invariant`), regenerated by `gen_tables.py` into the token tables. The one identifier in the tree
  (`generics.npk`'s `func:unbounded<U>`) is renamed `open_ended`; the libraries have none. Both interned as
  keywords everywhere: neither is a name after a `.` (unlike `acquire`/`any`), since neither is a member.
- **The clause on a loop**: `while (cond) decreases E invariant P { … }` and `while (cond) unbounded { … }`;
  the same on `when`, before its block. THE ORDER IS FIXED — the measure clause first, then `invariant`: the
  measure says WHY the loop ends, the invariant what holds while it runs, and one spelling is what the
  blueprint asks (a reader never has to remember which order a file chose). `decreases`/`unbounded` in any
  other position, twice, or both, is TYPE-072; on a `for`, `loop` or `till` — bounded by construction, D-234 and
  D-022 — TYPE-072 too, with the message saying so. The parser stores the measure in the loop statement's
  header as a `VerifyDecreasesNode` (kind 4, beside the three of 1.5.1) whose `b` is the expression or 0 for
  `unbounded`, through `stmt_while_decreases`/`stmt_when_decreases`; `stmt_expr_ids` (DEF-26's one enumeration
  of a statement's expressions) lists the measure, so every walker that must see into a statement sees it.
- **The clause on a function**: `decreases E` beside `requires`/`ensures` in the contract position, one at
  most (a second is TYPE-072), a `VerifyContractNode` with a new contract kind `CONTRACT_DECREASES`; `E` is
  over the parameters (as `requires` is), admitted as a contract expression (TYPE-060).
- **The measure's type**: a plain integer, `intN` or `uintN` — TYPE-073 otherwise (a float, a twisted kind, a
  bool, a struct). Below zero is a state a signed measure can reach and an unsigned one cannot; the check
  asks both of a signed measure and the strict decrease alone of an unsigned one.
- **`main`/`failsafe`** carry no contract (D-244) and take no function measure; their loops take clauses as
  any loop does.

### 2.2 The check in every build (step 1; D-304 §2)

- **A loop's check**, `emit_decreases_check`, mirrors `emit_invariant_checks` and runs at the HEAD, each time
  the condition holds, before the body (after the invariant's check — the invariant may bound the measure's
  own arithmetic): the measure `m` is evaluated (its guards — an overflow inside `E` — are the head's site, one
  row per context as DEF-81 lays it out), then `m < 0` traps (signed only), then `m >= prev` traps unless this
  is the first visit, then `prev := m`. `prev` and the first-visit flag live in ONE slot — the previous measure
  as an `i64`, with a sentinel that the domain never produces (DEF-69's rule): the flag is separate, an `i1`,
  never a magic number. Both are an alloca in a sync body and FRAME slots in a coroutine (a loop spans
  suspensions), reserved by the pre-scan at a new role beside 41's join marks — the number chosen at
  implementation from `ir_func.npk`'s table and recorded in the step's note. `continue` re-enters the head and
  is checked; `break` and `exit` leave without one. The trap is `DecreasesViolated`, 4119, through
  `emit_contract_trap`'s route; the check's site is the loop statement (the same key as the invariant's, the
  kind different).
- **A function's check**, at every call inside its recursive group (step 4, since it needs the groups): the
  measure is a generated predicate `<sym>.measure(params) -> i64` (widened by sign from the measure's type),
  the caller's checked entry stores `m0 = <sym>.measure(args)` into a slot (the split of D-252 already gives a
  function with a `requires` or a limited parameter a checked entry; a `decreases` splits it the same way), and
  every call site inside the group evaluates `m1 = <callee>.measure(actuals)` — the callee's measure over the
  argument registers, exactly as `.req` is called over them — and traps `DecreasesViolated` unless `m1 < m0`
  and `m0 >= 0`. A call to a function OUTSIDE the group is not a recursive call and checks nothing.
- **REACH** arms `DecreasesViolated` where a `decreases` is written (a loop's or a function's), walks the
  measure for its own arms (an overflow inside it), and arms nothing for `unbounded`.

### 2.3 The `terminate` rows and their elision (step 1; D-218 (7), D-219)

- **A loop's rows**, recorded by `walk_loop` beside the invariant's: the ENTRY row at the statement, goal
  `E >= 0` (signed measures; an unsigned measure's entry row is `true` and recorded discharged — the runners'
  `none` word); the PRESERVATION row at the body block, goal `E' < E` with `E'` the measure over the versions at
  the body's end and `E` over the versions at its start (the invariant and the condition are hypotheses inside
  the body, as they are for the invariant's preservation); one per `continue` that re-enters the loop. Kind
  `terminate`, SITE_STMT, the loop's clause context; `rows.txt`'s twelfth field carries it as it does for the
  invariant. The elision (`decreases_elided`) mirrors `invariant_elided`: every row of the loop discharged, and
  then the check's two compares become one `llvm.assume` each and the slot's store stays (a later visit's
  compare needs nothing, but the slot is a frame word and its liveness is the frame's business, not the row's).
- **A call's row** (step 4): at every call inside a recursive group, goal `callee.measure(actuals) <
  caller.measure(entry)` — the caller's measure a hypothesis at its entry (`>= 0`), the callee's measure an
  uninterpreted function of the actuals (D-242's `pure never fails` shape: the measure is a contract expression,
  so it IS a function of its arguments) — kind `terminate`, SITE_EXPR the call. A discharged call row elides
  the call site's compare.
- **What discharges.** A counter loop's measure `bound - v` against `v = v + 1`: `E' = E - 1 < E` outright,
  and `E >= 0` from the condition `v < bound` (1.5.4's path condition) — the 550 tool-written loops discharge
  as one shape. `n = n / 2` under `n > 0`: `E' = n / 2 < n` — the Int division form (D-279). A `List`'s
  `count` as the bound: `l.count - i` where `l` is address-taken has NO length term (DEF-14, E-4) — the row is
  `unencoded`, the check stays, and D-309's rule holds: reported, guarded, never a written bound.
- **Both runners**: `terminate` → 4119 in `TRAP_OF_KIND`, in `ASSUME_KINDS`; the kinds-agree check's "every
  guarded kind has a trap code" (1.5.8b step 3, K-12) holds it; the belts count the check's traps per loop —
  two per signed measure, one per unsigned — and one assume each where elided.

### 2.4 The recursive groups and the `stack-depth` rows (step 4; D-304 §§2, 5; D-305 (7))

- **The groups**: one new pass, `src/frontend/analysis/recursion.npk` — Tarjan's SCC over the call graph
  whose edges are the checker's recorded callees (`exprtypes_callee`, plus a plain call's identifier symbol —
  the resolution `reach_callee` performs since DEF-86, factored into a shared `call_target` both read), over
  `DeclFunctionDecl` nodes of the program's modules and the prelude alike. A call through a function value or a
  `dyn` is no edge — invisible by decision (TYPE-075's rationale in D-304). The pass records, per function, its
  group id and whether the group has a cycle (a self-call is one). `main` and `failsafe` are in no group.
- **TYPE-074**: if any member of a cyclic group states `decreases`, every member must, since a call from one
  member to another compares their two measures. **TYPE-075**: a `decreases` on a function whose group has no
  cycle checks nothing and is refused — the measure has no call to be compared at. Both at the declaration,
  naming the group's other members.
- **The `stack-depth` rows** (D-305 (7): reported, never eliding): one row per CYCLIC group, kind
  `stack-depth`, `discharged` when every member states `decreases` and every recursive call's `terminate` row
  is discharged — the depth is then bounded by the entry measure, a finite number the program computes — and
  `open` otherwise; recorded in `rows.txt` at the group's first member with no guard (the belts count no trap
  for it and no assume: it elides nothing, since D-305 keeps the stack check in every build). The manifest
  gains the compiler's own 103 rows, all `open`, since its recursion states no measure; that is the honest
  figure, and the residue report (D-309's shape, `residue.py`) lists them by group.

### 2.5 The sweep (step 3; D-304 §6)

- **The tool**, `meta/roadmap/1.5/tools/decreases_sweep.py`, kept beside the census: for every loop the PARSER
  finds (it drives `tools/parse_check.npk`'s AST dump, not a regex), with a head of the shape `v OP bound`
  (`<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=`, `!=` with a ±1 counter) whose counter is written in the body ONLY by `v = v ± 1`/`v ±= 1`
  and whose bound's variables are written NOWHERE in the body (no assignment, `@`, `$$m`, method call or move
  of any of them), it inserts `decreases bound - v` (ascending) or `decreases v - bound` (descending) after the
  condition, before any `invariant`; every other loop it lists with its file, line, condition and the writes it
  saw, sorted by class. The measure's spelling is the counter's own type (`bound - v` at the counter's width; a
  bound of another width is not a shape the tool writes). Expected from the census: ~550 written, ~300 listed.
- **The reading**, loop by loop from the tool's list, in the same step: each loop gets its measure — the idioms
  are named here so the reader has them: a halving loop `decreases n`; a scan `while (true)` that breaks on
  `pos >= len` — `decreases len - pos`, with the loop's own `pos < len` moved into the condition where the
  break was the only exit; a drain `while (work.count > 0)` that pops — `decreases work.count` is NOT sound if
  the body also pushes: read it, and where a drain can grow, `unbounded` with the reason; a retry loop over a
  budget `decreases budget - tries`; a flag loop `while (go)` — the flag is set by a computed condition, so the
  measure is that condition's counter, or `unbounded` where none exists; a `when` loop the same as a `while`.
  `unbounded` is written where and only where a loop may genuinely not end — the executor's run loop, the
  reactor's wait, a driver's dispatch, a read-to-end-of-input loop — each with a `//` reason on the line above.
  The prelude is swept with the tree (its loops are the compiler's own, D-205: a construct enters the prelude
  after a snapshot that understands it — step 2's).
- **The harness is the proof of the sweep**: every swept loop runs somewhere in the suite or in the compiler's
  own build, and a wrong measure traps `DecreasesViolated` the first time the loop's head sees it shrink or
  fall below zero. A trap is read, never re-measured: the loop's measure was wrong, or the loop does not end.
- **The library listener** is sent the tool and the idioms before step 3 lands (its ~loops need the same
  sweep before TYPE-072 goes live; F5 told it the rule).

> **Measured at step 1 (2026-09-24), with the tools written ahead of step 3** (`loop_dump.npk`, the
> parser-driven dump, and `decreases_sweep.py`, both beside the census): a DRY RUN over `src/`, `lib/`,
> `npkg/`, `tools/` and `tests/` writes **352** loops and lists **610** for a reader (15 already carry a
> clause: step 1's own tests). The written shape is the provable one and wider than §1's regex could see:
> a ±1 counter against a STABLE bound -- a literal, a plain name, a name's `.len`/`.count`, a widening
> cast, arithmetic over those, none of them written or address-taken in the body and the function -- and
> a top-level `&&` conjunction with exactly one such comparison. The reader's 610, by class: a call's
> result as the bound 134 (`while (i < (raw stmt_list_count(s)))` -- hoist the bound into a local, or

> unit step 82 + 23 (list walks `c = s.nodes[c].next`, skips), `while (true)` with a `break` 63 (scans),

### 2.6 What 1.5.8c does not do

- Inference of a measure as a RULE — declined by D-304 (a language rule cannot depend on how strong the solver
  is); the encoder may infer only to discharge a row, and the tool infers only to WRITE text a reader can see.
- A mandatory function measure — declined by D-304 §5 (D-305's stack check is the controlled stop).
- A frame condition for `List`-bounded loops (E-4, 1.6's leg B): their `terminate` rows are `unencoded` and their
  checks stay.
- NIKOS's disposition and the cycle's close: 1.5.8d.

## 5. Decisions taken at planning (each with its reason)

- **P-1 — the measure clause comes BEFORE `invariant`, and the order is fixed.** One spelling (the blueprint);
  the measure says why the loop ends, the invariant what holds — a reader meets the termination argument first.
- **P-2 — TYPE-072 is dormant until the sweep is landed (step 4), as D-304 §6 requires**, and the refresh
  between them is what lets `src/` carry the clauses (D-205). Three landings for one rule is the cost of a rule
  that touches 851 loops; landing them together would make a red run unreadable.
- **P-3 — the tool writes only the shape it can PROVE monotone** (a ±1 counter against a bound nothing in the
  body writes) and lists everything else. A measure the tool guessed and the harness never ran would be a
  silent lie in a safety clause; the reading list is the honest remainder.
- **P-4 — `unbounded` is written with its reason on the line above**, every time. The keyword is the opt-out;
  the reason is what a reviewer asks for next.
- **P-5 — the previous-measure slot and the first-visit flag are two slots**, never a sentinel value (DEF-69:
  a sentinel is a value the domain never produces, and an `i64` measure produces every `i64`).
- **P-6 — the `stack-depth` row is per cyclic group, reported, never eliding** — D-305 (7)'s words; it is
  `discharged` exactly when the group's measures make its depth a computed finite number.
- **P-7 — a call through a function value or a `dyn` is no edge of the call graph**, so a measure there is
  TYPE-075 (D-304's own rationale) and a recursion through one is D-305's stack check's to stop. Stated in
  VERIFICATION_REFERENCE §7b's `terminate` row so no reader expects otherwise.
- **P-8 — the census and the sweep tool live under `meta/roadmap/1.5/tools/`**, outside the TCB, as the
  explorer prototype does: they write and count SOURCE, and the compiler checks what they wrote.
